## Shape

**Four sections, always in this order.**

1. **Set A — taught in L1.** Table: Italian · English · example sentence · met
   in. The example sentence is compulsory and must be a sentence the learner
   could plausibly say about their own life, not a dictionary illustration. An
   item with no example sentence has been glossed, not taught.
2. **Set B — taught in L3.** Same columns.
3. **Recognition only.** Items that appear in the unit's input texts, are
   glossed, are **not** counted in the thirty, and are **not** tested at the
   checkpoint. Includes any receptive seeds (`piacere` chunks, congiuntivo
   chunks) with a one-line note saying they are deliberately unexplained.
4. **False-friend slot.** The unit's one item from the running false-friend
   strand: what it actually means, the English word it is not, and the Italian
   word that *does* mean the English one. Two minutes in L3, cumulative at both
   milestones.

**Three rules.**

- Items appear in the **citation form the learner should store** — infinitive
  for verbs, singular with article where gender is not predictable, masculine
  singular for adjectives. Matching `lexis-progression.md` exactly.
- Every item is **used** in at least one activity in the unit, not merely
  listed. The "met in" column is how that is checked: an item whose only entry
  is the lexis-presentation stage has not been taught.
- Counts must total the syllabus's thirty. If your split does not add to
  thirty, the split is wrong, not the syllabus.
