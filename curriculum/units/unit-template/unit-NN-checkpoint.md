## Shape

**Four parts, fixed** (ITA-4 §The four parts). Same in every unit, so a learner
always knows what they are walking into and a Unit 3 result can be compared with a
Unit 9 one:

| Part | Tests | Time | Marks | Format | Marked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Understanding | 3 min | 4 | One short text (60–90 words) **or** a script-read recording (40–60 s) — the parameter table says which. 4 items: 1 gist, 2 detail, **1 that cannot be answered without parsing the main structure** | Learner |
| **B** | Grammar in context | 3 min | 6 | One connected text, 70–90 words, **6 gaps**, no options: **4 on the main structure + 2 on the recycled structure** named in the parameter table | Learner |
| **C** | Lexis in use | 2 min | 5 | 5 items from **two subgroups** named in the parameter table: collocation completion, or odd-one-out with a one-word English reason. ≥ 1 chunk; the unit's false friend, if it has one, as **one** item | Learner |
| **D** | Productive turn | 2 min | 10 | Spoken 45–60 s recorded, **or** written 40–60 words (the parameter table says which). One situation, 2–3 required elements, in English, **never naming the structure**. TA 0–5 + TS 0–5 from [`rubrics.md`](../../../assessment/rubrics.md), with the unit's elicitation floor | Learner (TA) · partner (TS) |
| | | **10 min** | **25** | | 15 of 25 self-markable |

**Pass — all four conditions** (ITA-4 §Pass criterion): **≥ 18/25** · **TA ≥ 3** ·
**TS ≥ 3** · **≥ 1/2 on Part B's recycled gaps.**

**Six rules.**

1. **Ten minutes, timed.** If it takes longer it is a test, and this course does
   not test — it checks. In class, Part A is read aloud lockstep, B and C are
   silent, and Part D is recorded or written by everyone at once.
2. **Every objective item has a key and an alternatives list.** Self-study learners
   mark their own, so an item a reasonable learner could answer two ways without
   it being listed is a broken item, not a hard one. All-or-nothing; spelling and
   accents not marked unless the accent distinguishes the form.
3. **Nothing appears that was not taught and used in this unit or earlier.**
   Recognition-only items are not tested (lexis progression, §"Productive vs
   receptive"); before U12, nothing is tested as congiuntivo.
4. **The recycled gaps are not optional.** They come from a **bolded** row of
   the unit's L4 contract (Unit 1 excepted: within A2). Without them the spiral
   quietly stops being checked.
5. **A learner never scores their own TS.** A partner does. With no partner, the
   result is **provisional** and says so.
6. **The self-rating is part of the instrument**, not an afterthought. It is
   what a solo learner acts on.

**Marking and what follows.** Key with every answer. The result is written as a
**profile** — `A / B main + B recycled / C / D (TA, TS) / total / pass or not
yet` — never a bare total. Then the action table:

| Result | Reading | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass, 22–25 | Secure | Start the next unit. Bank the two items you got wrong. |
| Pass, 18–21 | Pass | Start the next unit, and redo the self-study for the weakest part before the next unit's L3. |
| Not yet — any condition missed | Not yet | **Start the next unit anyway.** Write a carry-forward entry: the structure, this unit, and the later L4 where it is already scheduled to return (ITA-4 §What a fail does instead). Redo the failed part's self-study before the next unit's L3. |

**A fail does not block progression** (ITA-4 §A fail does not block
progression). Never write "do not move on": the structure comes back at a
scheduled L4, and spacing does what repeating the unit would do worse. Escalation
— two consecutive *not yet* results trigger a repair set; three trigger a re-run
of the placement check — is ITA-4's, and the checkpoint quotes it in one line.

Then a **"where your marks were lost"** table pointing each part at the lesson
self-study that repairs it.

**Self-rating.** Each can-do goal, rated on the same three-point scale in every
unit: **1** not yet · **2** with support or notes · **3** unsupported.

**Last, the ITA-4 construct-validity checklist**, ticked for this unit, with
anything not yet verified (usually: the timing, until a learner has sat it) left
unticked and said so.

### Part B — Grammar in a text · 3 minutes · 6 marks

Chiara is writing to Petra, who is moving into her flat. Write the right form of
the word in brackets. One to three words per gap.

> Ciao Petra! Allora, ti racconto com'è la vita in questa casa. Io e Marta
> **(1)** ______ (svegliarsi) presto, alle sei e mezza, perché lavoriamo tutte e
> due in centro. La mattina io **(2)** ______ (farsi) la doccia per prima, e Marta
> prepara il caffè. Il giovedì lei **(3)** ______ (occuparsi) della spesa, e io
> pulisco la cucina. La sera io e Marta non **(4)** ______ (vedersi) molto, ma
> la domenica pranziamo sempre insieme. Le altre due ragazze sono molto
> **(5)** ______ (riservato), ma simpatiche. E tu, che lavoro **(6)** ______
> (fare)? Lavori anche tu in centro? A presto, Chiara

| Gap | Tests | Key | Not accepted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | riflessivi — `noi` | **ci svegliamo** | `*si svegliamo` |
| B2 | riflessivi — `io`, with an object | **mi faccio** | `*faccio` |
| B3 | riflessivi — third person | **si occupa** | `*mi occupa` |
| B4 | reciprocal | **ci vediamo** | `*si vediamo` |
| B5 | **recycled** — agreement | **riservate** | `riservata`, `riservati` |
| B6 | **recycled** — question form | **fai** (also `fai tu`) | `fa` (the letter is `tu`); `farai` (`Lavori…?` makes it present) |

*Four gaps on the main structure, two recycled, each answerable only from the
co-text (`Io e Marta`, `lei`, `ragazze`, `tu`). B3 is the L2 error-clinic error
turned into a gap: a learner who writes `mi occupa` has kept the drilled pronoun
instead of following the subject.*
