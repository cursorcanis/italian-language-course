## Shape

**Fixed proportions** (from `activity-repertoire.md` `A12`, and the syllabus
map's assessment section). Same in every unit, so a learner always knows what
they are walking into:

| Part | Tests | Marks | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | This unit's **lexis set** | 10 | Gap-fill or matching, no options given |
| **B** | This unit's **main grammar point** | 10 | Production, not recognition — the learner writes the form |
| **C** | **At least one recycled structure** from an earlier unit | 5 | Whatever format the structure calls for |
| | | **25** | |

**Five rules.**

1. **Ten minutes, solo, silent, timed.** If it takes longer than ten minutes it
   is a test, and this course does not test — it checks.
2. **Every item has a single unambiguous answer.** Self-study learners mark
   their own, so an item that a reasonable learner could answer two ways is a
   broken item, not a hard one.
3. **Nothing appears that was not taught and used in this unit.** Recognition-only
   items are not tested (lexis progression, §"Productive vs receptive").
4. **Part C is not optional.** A checkpoint with no recycled section lets the
   spiral quietly stop being assessed, which is how a spiralled course becomes
   a linear one.
5. **The self-rating is part of the instrument**, not an afterthought. It is
   what a solo learner acts on, and the only part of the checkpoint that
   changes what they do next.

**Marking and what follows.** Key with every answer. Then a three-band action
table, because a mark with no consequence teaches nothing:

| Mark | Reading | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 22–25 | Secure | Move on. Bank the two items you got wrong. |
| 18–21 | Pass | Move on, and redo the self-study for the one weakest part before the next unit's L3. |
| Below 18 | Not yet | Redo this unit's L2 self-study and the checkpoint in a week. Do not move on: the next unit builds on this one. |

**Self-rating.** Each can-do goal, rated on the same three-point scale in every
unit: **1** not yet · **2** with support or notes · **3** unsupported.

## Filled example (Unit 1, part B and its key)

### Part B — riflessivi and presente · 10 marks `[ITA?]`

Write the correct form of the verb in brackets.

1. La mattina io ______ (svegliarsi) alle sei e mezza.
2. Mia sorella non ______ (alzarsi) mai prima delle dieci.
3. Io e Marco ______ (sentirsi) ogni domenica.
4. Ragazzi, ______ (sbrigarsi)! Siamo in ritardo.
5. Prima di uscire, devo ______ (prepararsi).
6. I miei coinquilini ______ (occuparsi) del cane.
7. Il sabato ______ (rilassarsi, io) e non faccio niente.
8. Tu a che ora ______ (addormentarsi) di solito?
9. Noi ______ (vedersi) al bar dopo il lavoro.
10. Lei ______ (trovarsi) bene in questa città?

**Key.** 1 mi sveglio · 2 si alza · 3 ci sentiamo · 4 sbrigatevi · 5 prepararmi
(*or* mi devo preparare) · 6 si occupano · 7 mi rilasso · 8 ti addormenti ·
9 ci vediamo · 10 si trova. **1 mark each.**

*Item 5 accepts two answers because both are correct Italian and the lesson
taught both; that is a deliberate exception to rule 2, and the key says so
rather than leaving the marker to guess. Item 4 is the only imperative in the
paper: it was taught as a fixed chunk in L2 stage 7, and it is here because
`sbrigarsi` seeds U7.*
