| # | Can-do goal | Level | Owned by | Tested by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | I can introduce myself and describe my daily and weekly routine in enough detail that someone gets a real picture of my life, not just a list of times. | A2 | L1, L2 | Checkpoint B + the spoken production task |
| 2 | I can describe the people closest to me — what they're like and how I get on with them — using several different character adjectives. | A2 | L3 | Checkpoint A + the written production task |
| 3 | I can say what I like, need and miss using fixed expressions, even though I can't yet explain how they work. | A2 | L3 | **Not checkpoint-tested** — see the note below |

**The note on goal 3.** The `piacere` chunks are *recognition-only* lexis, and the
checkpoint rule is that recognition-only items are not tested
([`unit-template/unit-NN-checkpoint.md`](../unit-template/unit-NN-checkpoint.md),
rule 3). So goal 3 is assessed by **self-rating only**, and the self-rating
wording is adjusted to match: "I can use these five phrases without thinking
about them." This is the honest position. A goal phrased *"even though I can't yet
explain how they work"* cannot be marked out of five without testing the thing it
says is not yet the learner's job.

**Why `vedersi` and `sentirsi` sit in L2 and not with the rest of set B.** They
are the unit's grammar wearing different clothes: the same reflexive morphology
doing a *reciprocal* job. Teaching them as vocabulary in L3 would waste the one
place in the unit where a learner can see one form doing two things. The lexis
progression binds the split only to "set A in L1, set B in L3"
([`unit-template/README.md` §9](../unit-template/README.md#9-lexis-presentation--the-decision-and-why));
this carve-out is stated here so it is a decision and not a drift.

Outside the thirty: the five-chunk receptive `piacere` set (L3 s7, unanalysed).
False-friend slot (L3 s6): `i parenti` ≠ *parents*.

## 4. Grammar focus

- **Main — riflessivi (consolidation), with presente indicativo.** Taught in this
  order, because it is the order of *difficulty for an English L1 learner*, not
  the order a grammar book would use:
  1. the pronoun is **obligatory** — `mi sveglio`, never `*sveglio` for *I wake
     up*. This is the error that survives longest, so it is taught first and
     alone;
  2. the pronoun **agrees with the subject**, so it changes when the subject
     changes — including when the subject is `noi` and the meaning turns
     reciprocal;
  3. **placement** — before the conjugated verb; with a modal, **either**
     `mi devo alzare` **or** `devo alzarmi`, and never `*devo mi alzare`. Both
     correct options are taught as a pair, because teaching one as primary
     guarantees the learner invents the third.

  Alongside, as maintenance rather than instruction: the ten high-frequency
  present-tense irregulars (`essere, avere, fare, andare, venire, uscire, dovere,
  potere, volere, sapere`), all ten of which are in the L1 voice message.
- **Secondary — the `piacere` family as unanalysed chunks.** `mi piace / mi
  piacciono / mi interessa / mi serve / mi manca`, taught as fixed expressions
  and **explicitly not explained**. Deliberate seeding for U8
  ([`grammar-progression.md` §Seed 1](../../syllabus/grammar-progression.md#seed-1--piacere-family-u1--analysed-u8)).
  Seven units of exposure before the learner is asked to build it.
- **Deferred, and said out loud to learners who ask:** reflexives in the past
  (U2); `si` impersonale, which shares the surface form and will look like the
  same thing (U4); participle agreement with `farsi la doccia` (U6); why
  `piacere` works the way it does (U8).

## 5. Pronunciation / intonation point

**Word stress.** L3 stage 8, via `A9`.

- The **penultimate-syllable default**, stated once and then left alone.
- **`-ano` third-person plurals** — `àbitano`, `telèfonano`, `svégliano`,
  `si àlzano`, `si prepàrano` — where an English-L1 learner applies the
  penultimate default and produces *abitàno*. This is the case that matters,
  because the forms are high-frequency from the first week.
- **Final-stressed words**: `caffè`, `perché`, `università`.
- **Meaning-bearing pairs**, which is the test of whether a pronunciation point
  is worth a stage: `papà`/`pàpa` · `àbito`/`abìto` · `ancòra`/`àncora` ·
  `princìpi`/`prìncipi` · `sùbito`/`subìto`. Get the stress wrong in any of these
  and the listener hears a different word, not an accent.

## 6. Culture note

**`tu` and `Lei`, and how Italians actually negotiate the switch.** L4 stage 6,
via `A10`. Who offers `diamoci del tu` and who waits to be offered it; what
`dottore`, `ingegnere` and `professore` are doing; why an anglophone defaulting to
`tu` reads differently from an anglophone defaulting to first names in English.

- **Behavioural takeaway:** with someone you have not met and who is not obviously
  your peer, **start with `Lei` and let them downgrade it.** The cost of being
  slightly formal is nothing; the cost of the other error is real and invisible
  to you.
- **Sets up:** U7 (`Lei` imperatives), U11 (formal requests). It also establishes
  the register axis the whole course runs on, which is why it is in Unit 1 and not
  somewhere more convenient.

## 7. Production task

- **Spoken:** a two-minute audio self-portrait — who I am, what a normal week
  looks like, who is around me.
- **Written:** a language-exchange profile, ~120 words, the same ground, written
  for a reader rather than a listener.
- **Success criterion.** All three, countable, and applied by the learner to their
  own recording:
  1. runs **two minutes** with no stall longer than five seconds;
  2. uses at least **six** reflexive verbs correctly in the present;
  3. describes at least **two people**, each with a character adjective.
- **Scaffold and its removal.** L5 supplies the model (`A6` rung 1, cue 4) and a
  five-frame planner (rung 2). For the recorded attempt the planner is reduced to
  **five written words** (rung 3). The frames do not appear in the deliverable,
  and the learner is told to turn the page over — the removal is the part that
  makes the format work.

## 8. Checkpoint

10 minutes, solo, silent, run inside L5 stage 7. 25 marks: lexis 10, riflessivi
and presente 10, within-A2 recycling (question forms and adjective agreement) 5.
Pass criterion **18/25**, plus a self-rating against each of the four can-do
goals. Unit 1 is the one unit in the course with nothing earlier to recycle, so
its part C comes from **within** A2. Instrument and key:
[`unit-01-checkpoint.md`](unit-01-checkpoint.md).

**On the method column.** L1, L3 and L4 are **ESA**: nothing new is being
presented as form, so a Presentation stage would have nothing to present. L2 is
**PPP** because it has exactly one discrete structure to teach and its
*Presentation* is built out of what the group collected in L1's noticing grid —
guided discovery, not a paradigm table arriving from nowhere. L5 is **TBLT**: the
task is the lesson, and every form stage in the unit has been serving it.

## 10. What this unit recycles, and what it seeds

From [`grammar-progression.md` §Per-unit L4 contract](../../syllabus/grammar-progression.md#per-unit-l4-contract),
which is binding.

- **Recycles.** Nothing from earlier units — Unit 1 is the entry unit and the only
  one in the course with no spiral obligations. Its L4 therefore recycles *within*
  A2: **present-tense question forms** and **adjective agreement**. Both are
  chosen because the placement check assumes them and the unit's own can-do goal 4
  cannot be met without the first.
- **Seeds**, every one of which a later unit depends on:
  - the five `piacere` chunks, for analysis at **U8 L2** — seven units of
    exposure, and the course's proof case for receptive seeding;
  - `sbrigarsi`, whose imperative is needed at **U7**. It appears as an infinitive
    after a modal in L1 and as a fixed imperative chunk in L2 s7;
  - `farsi la doccia` — a reflexive with a direct object, which anticipates
    participle agreement at **U6**;
  - `il vicino`, which feeds **U3**'s building-and-neighbourhood set;
  - `sentirsi`, which is the same verb as *to feel* at **U8**;
  - the `tu`/`Lei` register axis, which **U7** and **U11** both build on.
