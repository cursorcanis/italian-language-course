## Stages in full

### 1 · Opener — *già fatto? non ancora!* — 5 min · pairs → lockstep

**Purpose.** Make learners want to say *already* and *not yet* before they have the
words, so stage 3 arrives as a solution.

**Instruction.** "With your partner. **Three things you've already done this week,
and one thing you haven't done yet but should have.** Italian where you can,
English for *already* and *not yet* if you need them. Ninety seconds each."

**CCQ.** "How many things you *haven't* done?" → *One.*

**Then, lockstep, 90 seconds.** "Who said *already* or *yet* in English?" Write
*already · just · not yet* on the right of the board, in English. Stage 3 crosses
them off.

**Correction stance.** None.

---

### 2 · Lexis set B (i) — time anchors on a timeline — 15 min · lockstep → solo

**Purpose.** Deliver eight of the ten time anchors **and** the unit's secondary point
— they are the same thing. Every event in this unit needs a *when*, and two of these
words (`da allora`, `finora`) quietly decide the tense.

**This is the heaviest stage in the lesson and the one that overruns.** Hold fifteen
minutes; the talking belongs in stage 8.

**Part 1 (6 min, lockstep).** Draw a timeline across the board: **ADESSO** at the
right-hand end. Elicit and place each item, with its example, saying it twice.

| Italian | English | Example — and the thing to say about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `due anni fa` | two years ago | `Mi sono trasferita due anni fa.` — **`[quantity] + fa`**, after, like *ago*: `tre mesi fa`, `una settimana fa`. |
| `l'anno scorso` | last year | **`scorso` goes after the noun and agrees with it**, like an adjective: `il mese scorso`, **`la settimana scorsa`**, `sabato scorso`. |
| `il mese scorso` | last month | `Il mese scorso ho fatto un colloquio.` |
| `di recente` | recently | `Di recente ho cambiato casa.` |
| `all'inizio` | at first | `All'inizio è stato difficile…` |
| `alla fine` | in the end | `…ma alla fine è andato tutto bene.` |
| `finora` | so far, up to now | **`Finora` + passato prossimo**: `Finora è andato tutto bene.` |
| `da allora` | since then | **`Da allora` + PRESENTE** when it is still true: `Mi sono trasferita ad aprile. Da allora abito a Milano.` |

**Part 2 (3 min, lockstep) — the one that matters most for an English speaker.**
Write the last row's pair big:

> `Mi sono trasferita ad aprile.` ← it happened, it's finished: **passato prossimo**
> `Da allora abito a Milano.` ← it's still true now: **presente**

"English says *since then **I've lived** in Milan*. Italian says *since then **I
live***. If it's still true now, Italian uses the present — the one from Unit 1."
Point back at Giulia [4]: `Da allora abito in un monolocale piccolissimo`.

**CCQ.** "`Da allora abito` — does she still live there?" → *Yes.* · "*Last week*:
`la settimana scorso` or `scorsa`?" → *`Scorsa` — `settimana` is feminine.* · "*Two
years ago*: `fa due anni` or `due anni fa`?" → *`Due anni fa`.*

**Part 3 (6 min, solo).** "Exercise 1 — seven gaps, five minutes. Then take your
timeline from Lesson 1 and **write one true sentence with `da allora`**: something
that happened, and what has been true since."

**Correction stance.** Accuracy, immediate: the position of `fa`, the ending of
`scorso`, and the tense after `da allora`.

### 3 · `già`, `appena`, `non ancora` — where they go — 11 min · lockstep → solo → pairs

**Purpose.** The last two set B (i) items and the unit's can-do goal 3. One rule,
and it is about **position**.

**Part 1 (4 min, lockstep).** Write three lines and cross *already · just · not yet*
off the English list:

> `Ho **già** finito il corso.` — already
> `Sono **appena** tornata.` — just
> **`Non`** `ho` **`ancora`** `trovato casa.` — not yet

"**Where's the little word?**" → *Between `ho` / `sono` and the long word.* "And
`non`?" → *Before `ho`.* "So the shape is always: `(non) + helper + già / appena /
ancora + participle`." Then two short answers, because they are what people actually
say: `Hai già mangiato? — **Sì, già.** / **Non ancora.**`

"With a reflexive, the little word from Unit 1 stays in front of everything:
`**Mi** sono **già** iscritta.` `**Non mi** sono **ancora** iscritta.`"

**CCQ.** "`Ho non ancora` or `non ho ancora`?" → *`Non ho ancora`.* · "*I've just
arrived*: `sono appena arrivato` or `appena sono arrivato`?" → *`Sono appena
arrivato`.*

**Part 2 (4 min, solo).** "Exercise 2: eight sentences, put the word in brackets in
the right place. The last three need a decision."

**Part 3 (3 min, pairs).** "Ask your partner **three** `Hai già…?` questions about
this week — `Hai già fatto la spesa?` `Hai già pagato l'affitto?`. Answer with
`Sì, già`, `Non ancora` or a full sentence."

**Correction stance.** Accuracy, immediate, on position.

---

### 4 · Reading — *Hai già trovato casa?* — 11 min · solo → pairs · `A1` shape, read

**Purpose.** Meet everything from stages 2–3 in a real exchange between two people
catching up — which is also can-do goal 4 in action: Tommaso keeps **following up**.
`A1`'s shape, shortened to two passes on a written text, because this lesson's form
has just been taught and needs no noticing pass.

**Instruction.** "A chat between two friends who haven't spoken for a year. **First
read: one question** — who has had the bigger change, Elisa or Tommaso? Two minutes.
**Second read:** exercise 3."

#### *Hai già trovato casa?*

> **Tommaso:** Elisa! Quanto tempo! Ho saputo che ti sei trasferita a Torino.
> Quando?
>
> **Elisa:** A marzo, sette mesi fa. All'inizio è stato difficile, ma da allora va
> sempre meglio.
>
> **Tommaso:** E hai già trovato casa?
>
> **Elisa:** Sì! Ho appena firmato il contratto per un bilocale in centro. Il
> trasloco è sabato.
>
> **Tommaso:** Grande! E il lavoro nuovo? Com'è andato finora?
>
> **Elisa:** Finora bene. Il mese scorso ho cominciato un progetto tutto mio.
>
> **Tommaso:** E il tedesco? Hai già fatto l'esame?
>
> **Elisa:** No, non ho ancora fatto l'esame: è a dicembre. Hai già visto le foto di
> Torino che ho messo online?
>
> **Tommaso:** Non ancora, stasera! Io invece mi sono appena iscritto a un corso di
> fotografia. E di recente ho fatto un colloquio a Milano.
>
> **Elisa:** Davvero? E com'è andato?
>
> **Tommaso:** Bene, credo. Ma non ho ancora saputo niente.
>
> **Elisa:** Allora incrociamo le dita! E quando vieni a Torino?

**Glossed:** `ho saputo` I heard (news) · `un bilocale` a two-room flat · `incrociamo
le dita` fingers crossed.

**CCQ.** "First read — how many questions?" → *One.*

**Expected gist answer.** **Elisa** — new city, new job, a flat she has just signed
for.

**Then, pairs, 3 minutes.** Compare exercise 3. Then one question to the room:
"**Find two places where someone asks a question about what the other person just
said.**" → *`E hai già trovato casa?` after `mi sono trasferita`; `E com'è andato?`
after `ho fatto un colloquio`.* "That's following up. It's stage 8's listener job."

**Correction stance.** Accuracy on exercise 3.

### 5 · Lexis set B (ii) — how it went — 7 min · lockstep → pairs

**Purpose.** The four evaluation items that turn a list of events into a story
someone wants to hear.

| Italian | English | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `soddisfatto` | satisfied, pleased | `Sono molto soddisfatta del nuovo lavoro.` |
| `deluso` | disappointed | `Il corso? Sono rimasto un po' deluso.` |
| `inaspettato` | unexpected | `È stata una svolta inaspettata.` |
| `valere la pena` | to be worth it | `Milano è cara, ma vale la pena.` |

**Instruction (3 min, lockstep).** Say each twice; learners repeat. "Three are
ordinary `-o` adjectives — U1's `riservato` group: `soddisfatta`, `delusi`,
`inaspettate`. `valere la pena` is a phrase: **`vale la pena`** — *it's worth it,
now* — is the form you need." (`ne è valsa la pena`, *it was worth it*, is a chunk in
the lexis file, recognition only.)

**Instruction (4 min, pairs).** "Exercise 4 — four gaps, one minute. Then tell your
partner **one thing from your year you're `soddisfatto` about, one you were `deluso`
by, and one thing that was `inaspettato`**. One sentence each."

**CCQ.** "A woman who is pleased: `soddisfatto` or `soddisfatta`?" →
*`Soddisfatta`.*

**Correction stance.** Accuracy on the endings in exercise 4; the pair talk is
fluency.

### 6 · False-friend slot — `la fattoria` — 3 min · lockstep

**Purpose.** The running strand: one item per unit, in L3, three minutes, every
time.

**Instruction.** "Three minutes, same as every unit. One word that looks like English
and isn't." Write it up in the fixed four-column shape:

| Item | Actually means | It is **not** | That word is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `la fattoria` | farm | *factory* | `la fabbrica` |

Then the evidence from L1, cue 1 [7]: *"Mia madre è andata a vivere in campagna, in
una vecchia fattoria con le galline."* "Can `fattoria` mean *factory* there?" → *No —
countryside, chickens.* Context, not the warning.

**Then exercise 5**, four items, sixty seconds.

**CCQ.** "*She works in a car factory* — `fattoria` or `fabbrica`?" → *`Fabbrica`.*

**Correction stance.** Accuracy, immediate.

### 7 · Pronunciation — double consonants — 14 min · lockstep → solo → pairs · `A9`

**Purpose.** Length, practised as **meaning-bearing**. The participles the unit runs
on — `fatto`, `detto`, `letto`, `scritto`, `successo` — have double consonants, and
in six everyday pairs a single for a double makes a different word.

#### Step 1 — Discriminate (5 min, lockstep)

**Instruction.** "Twelve words, six **pairs**. In each pair, one word has a **single**
consonant and one has a **double** — and they mean different things. You'll hear all
twelve in a mixed order. For each, write the **pair number and the letter**: `3A`,
`5B`. Exercise 6 part A."

Read cue 2 part 1, same speed and volume for every item.

**CCQ.** "What's different in each pair?" → *One consonant or two.* · "What do you
write?" → *Pair number and letter.*

Check immediately. "Who got ten or more?"

#### Step 2 — Mark (4 min, solo)

**Instruction.** "Exercise 6 part B. Ten sentences, each with one gap in a word —
**one letter or two?** Listen and write it. Here's the only rule you need: **a double
consonant is held, not louder — and the vowel before it is shorter.** `fat-to`:
short `a`, hold the `t` across the two syllables. `fa-to`: long `a`, quick `t`."

Read cue 2 part 2. Learners fill as they listen.

**CCQ.** "Double consonant — louder or longer?" → *Longer — held.* · "And the vowel
before it?" → *Shorter.*

Check in lockstep, 60 seconds. Then say the thing that makes it stick: "Look at the
participles you learned in Lesson 2: `fatto, detto, letto, scritto, rotto, successo,
messo` — **every one is double**. And `preso, perso, deciso` — **every one is
single**. The spelling you learned in L2 tells you how to say it."

#### Step 3 — Produce (5 min, pairs)

**Instruction.** "Pairs, part A in front of you. Say **one** word from a pair. Your
partner says which. Five each. If they can't tell, **hold the double longer** —
don't say it louder. Then read part B's sentences to each other, all ten."

**CCQ.** "If your partner gets it wrong — louder or longer?" → *Longer.*

**Correction stance.** Accuracy, immediate, generous — recast, don't explain.

### 8 · Activate — *il mio anno finora* — 20 min · lockstep → pairs → new pairs · `A6`

**Purpose.** The lesson's goal, performed: a year so far, every event placed in
time, with *already*, *just* and *not yet* — and a listener who follows up. The
scaffold comes off before the end.

#### Rung 1 — *I do* (3 min, lockstep)

Say this twice. Do not write it up.

> **Model.** "Mi sono trasferito a Bologna due anni fa. All'inizio è stato difficile,
> ma da allora mi trovo bene. L'anno scorso mi sono iscritto a un corso di cucina, e
> il mese scorso ho fatto il mio primo esame. Ho già trovato un lavoro part-time, ma
> non ho ancora trovato una casa più grande. E ho appena comprato una bici! Finora è
> andato tutto bene. Il corso è faticoso, ma vale la pena."

"What does every sentence have?" → *A when.* "And the three from stage 3?" → *`già`,
`non … ancora`, `appena`.*

#### Rung 2 — *we do* (9 min, pairs)

**Instruction.** "Your year so far, **out loud**, with the frames. Two and a half
minutes each. **Listener, two jobs:** at the end ask **one `Hai già…?` question**,
and then **one follow-up** about something they said — `E com'è andata?` `E adesso?`
`E poi?`" (`Com'è andata?` works for anything. Name the thing and it agrees:
`Com'è andato il colloquio?`, as in the chat.)

**Frames** (on the board):

1. `______ fa ______ .`
2. `All'inizio ______ , ma da allora ______ .`
3. `L'anno scorso / Il mese scorso ______ .`
4. `Ho già ______ , ma non ho ancora ______ .`
5. `Ho appena ______ .`
6. `Finora ______ .`

**Prepared lexis** (on the board): `soddisfatto/a · deluso/a · inaspettato/a · vale
la pena · è andato bene / male · mi trovo bene`

**CCQ.** "Listener — how many questions?" → *Two: one `Hai già`, one follow-up.* ·
"What's a follow-up?" → *A question about what they just said.*

**Correction stance.** Accuracy, narrowed: `già / ancora` position and the tense
after `da allora`. Collect two for stage 9.

#### Rung 3 — *you do* (8 min, new pairs)

**Rub the frames off.** Physically.

**Instruction.** "New partner. Frames gone. **Five words of notes, maximum.** Your
year so far, ninety seconds each. Listener: same two jobs."

**CCQ.** "How many words can you write?" → *Five.*

**Success criterion.** Without the frames, the speaker: (1) places **at least four**
events in time, with at least **three different** time expressions; (2) uses
**`già`**, **`non … ancora`** and **`appena`** at least once each, in the right
place. The listener asks **one `Hai già…?`** and **one follow-up**. Read it out
before rung 3.

**Correction stance.** Fluency, delayed, stage 9.

## Exercises and answer keys

### Exercise 1 — When?

Complete with: `fa` · `scorso` · `scorsa` · `da allora` · `finora` · `all'inizio` ·
`alla fine` · `di recente`. **Use each one once.**

1. Mi sono trasferito a Roma tre anni ______ .
2. La settimana ______ ho fatto un colloquio.
3. L'anno ______ mi sono iscritta all'università.
4. Mi sono laureata a giugno. ______ lavoro in un museo.
5. ______ è andato tutto bene: nessun problema.
6. ______ il corso è stato difficile, ma ______ ho capito tutto.
7. ______ ho cambiato casa: due settimane fa.

**Key.** 1 fa · 2 scorsa · 3 scorso · 4 Da allora · 5 Finora · 6 All'inizio … alla
fine · 7 Di recente. **8 marks** (item 6 has two). Accept `Finora` in 7? **No** —
`finora` means *up to now*, and `due settimane fa` is one point in time; that is the
distinction. *Why "use each one once":* without it, item 5 also takes `Alla fine`
or `Di recente` (`Alla fine è andato tutto bene` is good Italian), and the key would
have three answers.

*Item 4 is the one to talk about: `lavoro` is **present** because she still works
there. `Da allora ho lavorato` is the English *I've worked* translated, and it says
the job is over.*

### Exercise 2 — `già`, `appena`, `non … ancora`

Put the word(s) in brackets in the right place.

1. Ho finito il tirocinio. *(già)*
2. Siamo tornati dalle vacanze. *(appena)*
3. Ho trovato casa. *(non … ancora)*
4. Hai fatto il colloquio? *(già)*
5. Abbiamo deciso. *(non … ancora)*
6. Mi sono iscritta al corso. *(già)*
7. Luca si è laureato. *(appena)*
8. Mia sorella si è trasferita. *(non … ancora)*

**Key.** 1 Ho **già** finito il tirocinio. · 2 Siamo **appena** tornati dalle
vacanze. · 3 **Non** ho **ancora** trovato casa. · 4 Hai **già** fatto il colloquio?
· 5 **Non** abbiamo **ancora** deciso. · 6 Mi sono **già** iscritta al corso. · 7 Luca
si è **appena** laureato. · 8 Mia sorella **non** si è **ancora** trasferita.
**8 marks.**

*6–8 are the decisions: with a reflexive, the U1 pronoun stays in front of the
helper, `già` / `appena` / `ancora` still go after the helper, and `non` goes before
**everything** — `non si è ancora`, never `*si non è`.*

### Exercise 3 — *Hai già trovato casa?*: vero o falso?

1. Elisa si è trasferita a Torino sette mesi fa.
2. Elisa ha già trovato casa.
3. Elisa ha già fatto l'esame di tedesco.
4. Tommaso ha già visto le foto di Elisa.
5. Tommaso si è appena iscritto a un corso.
6. Tommaso ha già saputo com'è andato il colloquio.

**Key.** 1 **V** · 2 **V** — *ho appena firmato il contratto* · 3 **F** — *non ho
ancora fatto l'esame: è a dicembre* · 4 **F** — *non ancora, stasera* · 5 **V** ·
6 **F** — *non ho ancora saputo niente*. **6 marks.**

### Exercise 4 — How did it go?

Complete with `soddisfatto` / `deluso` / `inaspettato` (with the right ending) or `vale
la pena`.

1. Mia sorella ha trovato un lavoro bellissimo: è molto ______ .
2. Il film? Siamo rimasti tutti un po' ______ : il finale è brutto.
3. Il corso costa tanto, ma ______ .
4. Una telefonata ______ ha cambiato tutto.

**Key.** 1 soddisfatta · 2 delusi · 3 vale la pena · 4 inaspettata. **4 marks.**

### Exercise 5 — `fattoria` or `fabbrica`?

1. My uncle has a **farm** in Tuscany. → Mio zio ha una ______ in Toscana.
2. She works in a **factory** near Milan. → Lavora in una ______ vicino a Milano.
3. We bought eggs at the **farm**. → Abbiamo comprato le uova alla ______ .
4. The **factory** closed last year. → La ______ ha chiuso l'anno scorso.

**Key.** 1 fattoria · 2 fabbrica · 3 fattoria · 4 fabbrica. **4 marks.**

### Exercise 6 — Double consonants

**Part A — Discriminate.** You hear twelve words, both of every pair, mixed. Write
the pair number and A or B.

| Pair | A — double | B — single |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `anno` (year) | `ano` (anus) |
| 2 | `sonno` (sleepiness) | `sono` (I am) |
| 3 | `cassa` (till, checkout) | `casa` (house) |
| 4 | `sette` (seven) | `sete` (thirst) |
| 5 | `nonno` (grandfather) | `nono` (ninth) |
| 6 | `coppia` (couple) | `copia` (copy) |

**Key**, matching cue 2 part 1: 1 **4B** (sete) · 2 **1A** (anno) · 3 **6B** (copia)
· 4 **3A** (cassa) · 5 **2B** (sono) · 6 **5A** (nonno) · 7 **4A** (sette) · 8 **3B**
(casa) · 9 **1B** (ano) · 10 **6A** (coppia) · 11 **5B** (nono) · 12 **2A** (sonno).
**12 marks.** 10+ passes this step; below 8, repeat part A before part B.

**Part B — Mark.** One letter or two? Fill the gap.

1. Ho fa__o un colloquio.
2. È succe__o di tutto.
3. Hai le__o il mio messaggio?
4. Mi ha de__o di no.
5. Ho ro__o la lampada.
6. Ho scri__o a Mark.
7. Dove hai me__o le chiavi?
8. Ho pre__o il treno.
9. Mi sono trasferita due a__i fa.
10. Abbiamo trovato una ca__a nuova.

**Key.** 1 tt (fatto) · 2 ss (successo) · 3 tt (letto) · 4 tt (detto) · 5 tt (rotto)
· 6 tt (scritto) · 7 ss (messo) · 8 **s** (preso) · 9 nn (anni) · 10 **s** (casa).
**10 marks.** *8 and 10 are single — the two items that stop "always double" being a
winning strategy.*

**Part C — Produce.** Pairs: say one word from a pair, partner identifies; five
each. Then read part B aloud. **Success criterion:** your partner gets **4 of 5**.

### Exercise 7 — *Il mio anno finora* (homework, written)

About 100 words.

**Model answer:**

> Due anni fa mi sono trasferita a Leeds per lavoro. All'inizio è stato difficile,
> perché sono arrivata da sola. Da allora però mi trovo bene. L'anno scorso mi sono iscritta
> a un corso di italiano, e il mese scorso ho fatto un viaggio a Bologna con il mio
> compagno. Ho già trovato un appartamento più grande, ma non ho ancora fatto il
> trasloco: è a dicembre. Ho appena cominciato un corso di yoga, e sono molto
> soddisfatta. Finora è stato un anno pieno, ma vale la pena.

**What it has to do** — three things:

1. **At least four events**, each placed in time, with **three different** time
   expressions.
2. **`già`, `non … ancora` and `appena`**, once each, in the right place.
3. **One `da allora`** followed by the **present**.

*If a learner reaches for the imperfetto (`non conoscevo nessuno`), accept it and say
"Unit 4". The model avoids it on purpose; nobody is asked to produce it.*

## Teacher notes

Unit-level error profile and pacing:
[`unit-02-teacher-notes.md`](unit-02-teacher-notes.md).

**Predictable errors**

1. **`*Ho non ancora trovato casa`.** English *I have not yet found* maps word for
   word. Expect it in stages 3 and 8, and in writing. **Do:** point at the board line
   `Non ho ancora trovato casa` and say both aloud, wrong then right. The shape is
   `(non) + helper + ancora + participle`, and showing it beats saying it.
2. **`*Da allora ho abitato a Milano`.** English present perfect for something
   still true. It is not ungrammatical; it says the wrong thing — that she no longer
   lives there. Expect it in exercise 1 item 4 and stage 8 frame 2. **Do:** one
   question — "do you still live there?" — and let them switch to the present. L4
   comes back to it.
3. **`*fato`, `*leto`, `*scrito`.** The participle said with a single consonant and
   a long vowel. Understood from context, which is why it fossilises. **Do:** at
   stage 7 step 3 and in feedback, recast once, holding the double: "fa-**tt**o".
   Not before stage 7 — before it there is nothing to point at.

**On `ano`.** It is in the syllabus because it is the pair where the error is
**embarrassing rather than merely unclear**: `ho trent'ani` instead of `ho trent'anni`
is a joke every Italian teacher has heard. Say that, once, briefly, with a straight
face — it is the single most memorable reason to hold a double consonant, and adults
can take it. If your group or setting makes that wrong, swap pair 1 for `carro / caro`
(*cart, float / dear, expensive*). **Approved by the Content Editor (ITA-21).** Both
are real words, the pair is meaning-bearing, and `caro` is a word learners need.
Pair 1 becomes **A `carro` · B `caro`**. In cue 2 part 1, item 2 becomes `carro` and
item 9 becomes `caro`, and the key letters (`1A`, `1B`) do not change. The
recording, once made, will carry `anno / ano`, so the swap works only when you read
cue 2 aloud yourself. Part B is unaffected: item 9 is `anni`.

**The `piacere` seed.** `piacere` chunks are recognition-only until `U8 L2`; do not
explain the construction. This lesson's texts contain none; if a learner produces
`mi è piaciuto` in stage 8, accept it as the phrase from L1 and say nothing.

**Correction stance per stage.** Stages 2, 3 (parts 1–2), 4, 5 (exercise), 6, 7 —
**accuracy**, immediate; stage 7 generous and by recast. Stages 1, 3 (part 3), 5
(pairs), 8 rung 3 — **fluency**, delayed, stage 9. Stage 8 rung 2 — accuracy,
narrowed.

**To shorten (−11 min).** Run stage 4 as one pass with exercise 3 only (−4), drop
stage 5's pair talk (−3), and run stage 8 rung 2 for six minutes, not nine (−3), and
take a minute off stage 1. Do **not** cut stage 7 — it is the unit's only
pronunciation stage — and do **not** cut stage 8 rung 3: it is the only place goal 3
is performed without the scaffold.

**To extend (+12 min).** After stage 8, an `A5` mingle: *trova qualcuno che… ha già
prenotato le vacanze / si è appena trasferito / non ha ancora finito un libro
cominciato quest'anno / ha fatto un colloquio di recente*, one compulsory follow-up
per person. It rehearses L4's activity in miniature.

## Self-study version — 50 minutes

| Live stage | Solo equivalent | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Opener (pairs) | Say **out loud** three things you've already done this week and one you haven't. Write down the English words you needed. | 3 |
| 2 Time anchors (lockstep → solo) | Draw the timeline; copy the eight items onto it with their examples, saying each aloud. Copy the `da allora` pair. Then exercise 1 and your own `da allora` sentence. | 10 |
| 3 `già / appena / non ancora` (lockstep → solo → pairs) | Copy the three board lines. Exercise 2. Then ask **yourself** three `Hai già…?` questions about this week, aloud, and answer each aloud. | 7 |
| 4 Reading (solo → pairs) | Identical: gist first, then exercise 3, then find the two follow-up questions. | 8 |
| 5 Evaluation lexis (lockstep → pairs) | Exercise 4. Then write your three sentences — `soddisfatto`, `deluso`, `inaspettato` — about your year. | 5 |
| 6 False friend (lockstep) | Exercise 5. Add `fattoria ≠ factory` to your false-friend page under `parenti`. | 2 |
| 7 Pronunciation (lockstep → solo → pairs) | Read cue 2 aloud yourself for part A, then part B. Step 3 becomes **record-and-compare**: record five pairs and the ten part B sentences, listen back, mark where your doubles were not held. Twice. | 8 |
| 8 Activate (`A6`) | Rung 2 **written**, all six frames. Rung 3 **spoken and recorded**, frames covered, five words of notes, 90 seconds. Then answer, aloud, two questions you set yourself in advance: one `Hai già…?`, one `E com'è andata?`. | 5 |
| 9 Delayed feedback | Listen once: **did `già` and `ancora` land between the helper and the participle every time?** Write any sentence where they didn't, corrected. | 2 |
