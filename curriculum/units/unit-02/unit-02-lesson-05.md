## Stages in full

### 1 · Opener — your forty seconds from Lesson 1 — 6 min · solo → pairs

**Purpose.** Four lessons ago, before the past was taught, every learner recorded
forty seconds of *il mio anno*. Hearing it now is the unit's most motivating six
minutes.

**Instruction.** "Phones out, one earphone in. **Listen to your forty seconds from
Lesson 1.** Write two things: **one sentence you'd now say differently** — write
how — and **one thing you didn't say because you couldn't.** Three minutes."

**CCQ.** "Are you re-recording it?" → *No — this is the before.*

**Then, pairs, 2 minutes.** "Tell your partner your two things."

**If a learner has no recording:** "Write, from memory, five past forms you know now
and didn't four lessons ago." Same three minutes.

**Correction stance.** None.

---

### 2 · Task framing and the model — 12 min · lockstep → solo · `A6` rung 1 `[ITA?]`

**Purpose.** State the task and the criterion, then play a model that **clears** the
criterion without showing off.

**Part 1 (3 min, lockstep).** Write this up and leave it all lesson:

> ### Il mio anno — spoken
> **Tell three things that happened this year. Then answer two questions.**
>
> **Done means all three:**
> 1. **every event has a *when*** — a month, `fa`, `scorso`…;
> 2. **eight or more** passato prossimo verbs in the whole turn, **at least two with
>    `essere`**, ending right;
> 3. **both questions answered**, two sentences or more each.
>
> **The asker's job:** two follow-up questions about **what was just said** — not a
> new topic.

"Those three are the whole assessment for the spoken part, and the asker has one job.
You can do this with the Italian you had at the end of Lesson 4."

**Part 2 (4 min, lockstep).** "Listen to Luca — Giulia's brother, from Lesson 1. Three
things, then a friend asks two questions. **Exercise 1 while you listen**: what are
his three things, and what are the two questions?"

Read or play cue 3. **Time it** and say the time: "One minute thirty. Three things
plus two answers is a minute and a half. Shorter than you think."

**Part 3 (5 min, solo → check).** "Exercise 1 part B: **did he meet the three
criteria? Count.**"

**CCQ.** "Did the friend ask about something new, or about what Luca said?" → *What
Luca said.* · "How many past verbs do you need?" → *Eight.*

**Correction stance.** Accuracy on exercise 1.

---

### 3 · The planner — 11 min · solo → pairs · `A6` rung 2 `[ITA?]`

**Purpose.** The scaffold, filled before anyone speaks — and a partner's guess at
the follow-ups, so the answers are not cold.

**Instruction (7 min, solo).** "Exercise 2 — the planner. **Notes, not sentences.**
Row 4 is your L4 svolta if you want it. Row 6: write two questions you think you'll
be asked, and two words of answer for each."

**The planner**

| # | Row | Your notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Cosa 1** — quando? che cosa è successo? | |
| 2 | **Cosa 2** — quando? | |
| 3 | **Cosa 3** — quando? | |
| 4 | Com'è andata? `soddisfatto · deluso · inaspettato · vale la pena` | |
| 5 | **E adesso?** — one thing true now (present) | |
| 6 | Two questions I might get — and two words of answer each | |

**Instruction (4 min, pairs).** "Swap planners. **Read your partner's silently and
write one question you'd actually ask** at the bottom. Then swap back."

**CCQ.** "Notes or sentences?" → *Notes.* · "When you get your partner's planner —
correct it, or ask about it?" → *Ask about it.*

**Correction stance.** None on notes. Check only that **every row has something**,
and that rows 1–3 each have a *when*.

---

### 4 · Rehearsal 1, timed — 12 min · pairs · `A11` attempt 1

**Purpose.** Perform once against the scaffold and **find out** — the diagnostic,
not the deliverable.

**Instruction.** "Pairs. **A goes first**, planner in front. Tell your three things.
B has four jobs, only these:
1. **time it**;
2. **count the past verbs** on your fingers — you need eight — and say how many had
   `essere`;
3. **tick each event that had a *when***;
4. at the end, **ask two follow-up questions about what A said**.
A answers both. Then swap. Five minutes each."

**CCQ.** "B — are you correcting A's Italian?" → *No — timing, counting, ticking,
and two questions.* · "B's questions — about anything?" → *About what A just said.*

**Correction stance.** Fluency. Say nothing. Collect six things for stage 9's close.

---

### 5 · Two feedback points, named — 6 min · pairs → lockstep

**Purpose.** `A11`'s hinge: **two** points before attempt 2, from the criterion.

**Instruction (4 min, pairs).** "Exercise 3 — the sheet. Tell your partner their
numbers. Then **one thing that met the criterion**, and **one thing to fix** —
**from the three on the board**, nothing else. Write both on their planner."

**CCQ.** "How many things to fix?" → *One.* · "Can it be any mistake you heard?" →
*No — one of the three criteria.*

**Then, lockstep, 2 minutes.** "Hands up: who was under eight past verbs?" Usually
many. Say: **"The fix is almost never more Italian. It's row 4 and row 5 — how it
went, and how it is now. Each one is two more verbs."**

**Correction stance.** Delayed and selective, by design.

---

### 6 · The recording — 14 min · new pairs · `A11` attempt 2 · `A6` rung 3

**Purpose.** The deliverable. Same task, **scaffold removed**, one named fix in hand,
and **a new asker** — so the follow-ups are real questions, not rehearsed ones.

**Instruction.** "**Turn your planner face down.** On a new piece of paper you may
write **five words**. New partner. Speaker: phone recording — three things, then
your partner asks two questions and **you answer into the same recording**. Asker:
two follow-ups about **what you just heard** — `E com'è andato?` `E adesso…?` `Ti
sei pentito?`. Then swap. Ten minutes; you may record twice."

Write on the board: **`5 parole · 3 cose · 2 domande`**.

**CCQ.** "Is the planner in front of you?" → *No — face down.* · "Asker — can you ask
the questions from their planner?" → *No — about what I just heard.*

**Turning the planner over is the stage.** Walk round and check.

**Success criterion.** The three on the board, applied by the learner to their own
recording with exercise 4. **The asker** succeeds if both questions pick up something
the speaker said.

**No device?** Perform live to the partner, who times and counts; they say which
criteria were met. Arranged at the door.

**Solo learners** — and any pair short of an asker — use cue 4 as the asker: play it
after the three things, answer into the pause.

**Correction stance.** None.

---

### 7 · Checkpoint — 10 min · lockstep → solo, timed · `A12`

**Purpose.** The unit check, built to the ITA-4 instrument so a Unit 2 result can be
compared with any other unit's.

**Three blocks, and you hold the clock:**

1. **Part A, silent (3 min).** "Papers over. **Part A is a message to read** — three
   minutes for the message and four questions. English or Italian answers."
2. **Parts B and C, silent (5 min).** "Parts B and C, alone, no notes. Five minutes.
   I'll call one minute."
3. **Part D, everyone at once (2 min).** "Part D is **written**: read the prompt,
   write forty to sixty words. Two minutes." Call *start* and *stop*.

**CCQ.** "Notes?" → *No.* · "Is it a test?" → *No — you mark most of it, and it
tells you what to do next.* · "Part D — speaking or writing?" → *Writing.*

Instrument and key: [`unit-02-checkpoint.md`](unit-02-checkpoint.md). **Stop each
block on time**, finished or not.

**Correction stance.** Accuracy; the marking is the learner's.

---

### 8 · Mark, self-rate, act — 9 min · solo → pairs → solo

**Instruction, part 1 (4 min, solo).** "Keys are in the file. **Mark A, B and C.**
Then score your Part D for **TA only** — the three required elements and the length.
Leave TS empty."

**Instruction, part 2 (2 min, pairs).** "Swap papers. Read your neighbour's Part D
**once**. Your only job: **count the passato prossimo verbs, and how many have the
right helper and ending.** Five or more, most right, at least one `essere` — that's a
3. Write their TS."

**CCQ.** "What do you score for your partner?" → *Only the past verbs.*

**Instruction, part 3 (3 min, solo).** "Three things: write your **profile** and
check the **four** pass conditions; find your row in the table and **write down what
it tells you**, with a carry-forward entry if it says *not yet* — **everyone starts
Unit 3**; and the self-rating, four goals, **1, 2 or 3**."

**Correction stance.** Self-marked, except TS.

### 9 · The written task, and the unit close — 10 min · lockstep `[ITA?]`

**Part 1 — the written task (4 min).** "The other half, as homework. **A 150-word
post: *il mio anno*, for a blog or a social feed.** Exercise 5 has a model and the
four things yours has to do. The one that's new: **it has to read as a story, not a
list** — three linking expressions at least. You did that in Lesson 4 with your
svolta."

**Part 2 — the close (6 min).** The four can-do goals on the board, one by one, a
minute and a half each. Ask which goal most people rated lowest:

| # | Goal | Where you did it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Say what I've done this year and place each event in time | Today's recording. Compare it with your forty seconds from Lesson 1. |
| 2 | Tell a significant change in five to eight connected sentences | L4's svolta, and the written post's linking criterion. |
| 3 | Say whether something has already, just, or not yet happened | L3. On the checkpoint in Part C. |
| 4 | Ask about someone's recent past and follow up | L4's survey and svolta; today's asker role. **Self-rated** — no ten-minute item can test a follow-up. A 1 or 2 is normal; it needs a person, and Unit 3 keeps asking. |

Then the one sentence that frames Unit 3, said exactly: **"Next unit is where you
live. And the first thing anyone asks about a flat is *how did you find it?* — `ho
trovato`, `mi sono trasferito`, `abbiamo visto tre appartamenti`. The past comes with
you."**

**Correction stance.** None.

## Exercises and answer keys `[ITA?]`

### Exercise 1 — *Tre cose del mio anno*: the model

**Part A.** What are Luca's three things? What are the friend's two questions?

**Key.** Three things: **(1)** he **graduated** in July — engineering, after seven
years; **(2)** he **found a job in Turin** in October and **moved** in November;
**(3)** his **parents separated** in the spring. Questions: **`E come ti trovi a
Torino?`** · **`E tua madre? È ancora in campagna?`** **5 marks** (3 + 2).

*Both questions pick up something Luca said — Turin, his parents. That is what a
follow-up is.*

**Part B.** Did Luca meet the three criteria? Count.

1. Did every event have a *when*? Which words?
2. How many passato prossimo verbs? How many with `essere`?
3. Did he answer both questions with two sentences or more?

**Key.** 1 **Yes** — `a luglio`, `a ottobre`, `a novembre`, `in primavera`, `il mese
scorso`, `a dicembre`. · 2 **Twelve**: `mi sono laureato`, `ho ballato`, `ho trovato`,
`ho fatto`, `ha scelto`, `mi sono trasferito`, `è stato`, `si sono separati`, `è
stato`, `mi sono iscritto`, `è rimasta`, `sono andato` — **eight with `essere`**.
Accept any count of eight or more with two `essere`. · 3 **Yes** — three sentences
and two. **3 marks.**

### Exercise 2 — The planner

The six-row table in stage 3. **No key.** **Success criterion for the planner:** all
six rows have notes, rows 1–3 each have a *when*, and row 5 is in the present.

### Exercise 3 — The feedback sheet

Fill this in **about your partner**, after rehearsal 1.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| How long they spoke | ______ |
| Past verbs counted | ______ (need 8) · with `essere`: ______ (need 2) |
| Events with a *when* | ______ of 3 |
| Did both answers have two sentences? | yes / no |
| **One thing they did well** — which criterion? | |
| **One thing to fix** — must be one of the three criteria | |

**Marking guidance.** The fix must be **one** of the three criteria. "Your `gli`" is
not feedback here: it cannot be acted on in ten minutes.

### Exercise 4 — The spoken task: rubric

Apply this to **your own recording**, listening back once.

| Criterion | Met? | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Every event has a *when* | | ______ of 3 |
| 2. Eight+ PP verbs, two+ with `essere`, endings right | | Count: ______ · `essere`: ______ |
| 3. Both questions answered, two sentences each | | |

| | Reading | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **All three met** | The unit's goal is met. | Keep both recordings. |
| **Two of three** | A pass, and the normal first outcome. | Record once more this week, fixing only the missing one. |
| **One or none** | Not yet — almost always criterion 2, too few verbs. | Don't re-record yet. Add notes to planner rows 4 and 5 — how it went, how it is now — and record from the fuller planner. **Too few verbs is a content problem, fixed on paper.** |

### Exercise 5 — The written task (homework)

A 150-word post, *il mio anno*, for a blog or a social feed.

**Model answer** (~150 words)

> **Il mio anno, in breve** — *Sam, Manchester*
>
> Che anno! A gennaio mi sono iscritto a un corso di italiano, il martedì sera — e
> sì, il martedì devo ancora sbrigarmi. A marzo io e Tom ci siamo trasferiti in un
> appartamento più grande, vicino al parco. Il trasloco è stato un incubo, ma vale
> la pena.
>
> Poi, a giugno, sono andato in Italia per la prima volta: dieci giorni a Bologna.
> All'inizio ho capito poco, ma alla fine ho parlato italiano anche al mercato! Ad
> agosto Tom ha cambiato lavoro, e così a settembre siamo partiti per una settimana
> in Sicilia, per festeggiare.
>
> Il mese scorso ho fatto il mio primo esame di italiano. Non ho ancora saputo il
> risultato… incrociamo le dita! Da allora studio un po' ogni giorno.
>
> E voi? Com'è andato il vostro anno?

**Rubric** — four rows, 2 marks each, 8 marks:

| | Criterion | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Passato prossimo** — 8+, at least 2 with `essere` and agreement | 8+, both `essere` agreeing | 8+, one agreement slip | Fewer than 8, or no `essere` |
| 2 | **Time** — every event placed | Every event | All but one | Two or more unplaced |
| 3 | **Connected** — 3+ linking expressions; reads as an account | 3+, reads as a story | 3, but still list-like | Fewer than 3 |
| 4 | **The post** — ~150 words (130–170), an opening and a closing line | Both, in range | One missing, or out of range | Neither |

**6/8 is a pass.** The model scores 8: ten PP verbs (five with `essere`); every event
placed; linking with `ma`, `poi`, `all'inizio`, `alla fine`, `così`, `da allora`.

**Linking expressions, for this task:** `poi · dopo · ma · però · perché · quindi ·
così · all'inizio · alla fine · da allora · finora`. Any three distinct ones count.
(The narrative connectives — `a un certo punto`, `insomma` — are Unit 5's.)

*One thing to say about the model, out loud, when you hand back the homework:
`Da allora studio` is the present for something still true — L3 and L4 — and the
first line about Tuesdays is Unit 1's `sbrigarsi`, still there a unit later.*

## Teacher notes

Unit-level error profile and pacing:
[`unit-02-teacher-notes.md`](unit-02-teacher-notes.md).

**Predictable errors** — two of the three are not language errors, which is normal
for a TBLT lesson.

1. **Too few past verbs on attempt 1** — five or six, not eight. The learner tells
   three events in one clause each. **Do:** the stage 5 sentence — "rows 4 and 5" —
   and in feedback, name **which row** to grow. Never "use more verbs".
2. **Follow-ups that change the subject.** The asker asks a prepared question —
   `Hai figli?` — instead of picking up what was said. Goal 4 is exactly the
   difference. **Do:** at stage 4, after the first pair, say to the room: "A
   follow-up starts from **their** last sentence." The new partner in stage 6 is the
   structural fix: they have not seen the planner.
3. **`essere` agreement collapsing under load.** `*mia sorella si è trasferito`,
   right in L2 and lost here, because attention is on the clock. **Do:** nothing,
   during. It costs under criterion 2 when the learner counts, and in rubric row 1 of
   the written task — which is where it should cost.

**The `piacere` seed.** `piacere` chunks are recognition-only until `U8 L2`; do not
explain the construction. If `mi è piaciuto` appears in a recording or the post,
it is the L1 phrase: it counts as neither a past verb for criterion 2 nor an error.

**Correction stance per stage.** Stages 2 and 7 — **accuracy**. Stage 8 —
self-marked, TS by a partner. Everything else — **no correction**, by design.

**To shorten (−12 min).** Cut stage 1 (−6); planner to 8 minutes (−3); close to 7
minutes (−3). **Never shorten stage 6**, and **never move the checkpoint out of the
lesson**.

**To extend (+12 min).** A listening gallery after stage 8: groups of three swap
phones and score each other's recordings against exercise 4 — and, for each, write
one *more* follow-up question they would have asked.

## Self-study version — 60 minutes

**Do it in one sitting.** The gap between attempt 1 and attempt 2 is where the
improvement happens.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Opener (solo → pairs) | Identical listening; **write** the two answers. | 5 |
| 2 Task framing + model | Copy the three criteria onto a card. Read the cue 3 script aloud, both voices, **timing yourself**. Exercise 1, key supplied. | 9 |
| 3 Planner (solo → pairs) | All six rows, notes. Then put it away for two minutes, come back, and write **one question a stranger would ask** at the bottom. | 8 |
| 4 Rehearsal 1 (pairs) | Record attempt 1, planner in front. Then **play cue 4** and answer two of its questions into the same recording. Listen back once doing the partner's jobs: time, count verbs and `essere`, tick the *when*s. | 10 |
| 5 Two feedback points | Exercise 3 on yourself. **One** fix, from the criteria; if it's verb count, name the planner row. | 3 |
| 6 The recording (new pairs) | Planner face down, five words, record. After your three things, **play cue 4 from a different starting question than in stage 4** — the questions you have not heard yet are the nearest thing to a new asker. Answer into the recording. Then exercise 4. | 11 |
| 7 Checkpoint (solo, silent) | **Ten minutes, timed, no notes.** Parts as written; Part D written. | 10 |
| 8 Mark, self-rate, act | Mark A–C and your TA. **TS: leave blank, write *provisional*** — and since Unit 1's may also have been provisional, read the checkpoint file's note on finding a marker. Then the action row, the carry-forward entry if *not yet*, and the self-rating. | 4 |

## After the lesson

- **Homework.** Exercise 5 — the 150-word post, self-marked against its four-row
  rubric. (~30 minutes.)
- **Keep three things:** the forty seconds from L1, today's attempt 1 and attempt 2.
  Milestone 1 asks for them.
- **Act on the checkpoint row before Unit 3's L3.** A *not yet* means starting Unit 3
  anyway, with a carry-forward entry — the passato prossimo returns at U3 L4
  (`ho trovato`, `mi sono trasferito`) as an extra encounter and at **U6 L4** as its
  mandated one.
- **Unit 3** is where you live — and the past comes with you.
