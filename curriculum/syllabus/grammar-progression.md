# Grammar Progression — A2 → B1

**Companion to [`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md`](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md).** The map says
what each unit teaches. This document says, for every structure in the course,
**where its three encounters are** — located by unit *and lesson slot*, so that
"we recycle it later" is a checkable claim rather than an intention.

> **Status:** all Italian in this file — forms, chunks, illustrative fragments —
> is a *specification* of what the language must do. It is pending sign-off by
> the Italian Content Editor before any of it reaches a learner. See
> [`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md` §6](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md#6-what-this-map-deliberately-does-not-decide).

## How to read this document

### The three-encounter rule

Every taught structure gets **three planned encounters**, in three *different*
themes:

| # | Encounter | Slot | What has to happen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Introduced** | `L2` (heavy) or `L3` (light) | Analysed and practised under control. Always preceded by receptive exposure in that unit's `L1`. |
| **2** | **Recycled** | a **later** unit's `L4` | Re-worked inside a *different* theme, usually with a new job to do. Not re-explained from scratch. |
| **3** | **Freely produced** | a **still later** unit's `L5`, or **Milestone 2** | Used unsupported in the production task, with no scaffold naming the structure. |

A structure introduced at `U6 L2` and never named again in a later `L4` has one
encounter, not three, and the learner will lose it. That is the failure this
document exists to make visible.

### Notation

- `U6 L2` = Unit 6, lesson 2. `M2` = Milestone 2, after Unit 12.
- **Spacing** is the gap in units between encounters, written `intro→recycle,
  recycle→free`. Under **distributed practice** these should widen. Where they
  do not, it is called out rather than hidden.
- *(cons.)* = consolidation of A2 material the learner arrives half-owning.
  *(new)* = new at this level.
- **Extra encounters** are real additional exposures on top of the mandated
  three. They are a bonus, never a substitute for a missing one.

### What is binding on whom

- **Lesson Designer (ITA-5):** the `L4` column of [§Per-unit L4
  contract](#per-unit-l4-contract) is a contract. An `L4` that drops its listed
  structures breaks the spiral for every unit downstream.
- **Assessment framework (ITA-4):** every structure whose third encounter is
  `M2` must be *elicited* by Milestone 2, not merely permitted by it. See
  [§What this hands to ITA-4](#what-this-hands-to-ita-4).
- **Italian Content Editor:** the receptive-seeding lists in
  [§Receptive seeding](#receptive-seeding) are input-text requirements — the
  chunks have to actually be in the texts.

## Receptive seeding

Referenced from [`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md`
§5](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md#5-deliberate-compression-at-the-end-of-the-course-stated-plainly).

**Input before output**, applied across units rather than within one. A
structure that will be analysed late is planted early as *unanalysed chunks* in
input texts: recognition-only, never a production target, never explained, never
tested. By the time it is analysed the learner has met the pattern dozens of
times and is confirming something rather than learning it cold.

This is a requirement on the Italian Content Editor's input texts, not a
suggestion: if the chunks are not in the texts, the seeding does not exist.

### Seed 1 — `piacere` family (U1 → analysed U8)

Planted `U1 L3` as five fixed chunks; recurs in every lexis set from U2 to U7;
analysed at `U8 L2`.

| Chunk | First seeded | Analysed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mi piace` / `mi piacciono` | `U1 L3` | `U8 L2` |
| `mi interessa` | `U1 L3` | `U8 L2` |
| `mi serve` | `U1 L3` | `U8 L2` |
| `mi manca` | `U1 L3` | `U8 L2` |
| `mi sembra` | `U2 L1` | `U8 L2` |

Seven units of exposure. This is the proof case: it demonstrably works, which is
why Seed 2 copies it.

### Seed 2 — congiuntivo presente (U5 → analysed U12)

Planted from `U5 L1` onward as recognition-only chunks in input texts. Listed in
unit glossaries as fixed expressions with an English gloss, **flagged
recognition-only**, and excluded from every checkpoint before U12.

| Chunk | Seeded from |
| --- | --- |
| `penso che sia` | `U5 L1` |
| `spero che vada` | `U5 L1` |
| `può darsi che` | `U7 L1` |
| `sembra che sia` | `U8 L1` |
| `bisogna che` | `U9 L1` |
| `credo che abbia` | `U10 L1` |
| `mi sa che` *(indicative, but the same hedging job)* | `U6 L1` |

Seven units of exposure before `U12 L2`, matching Seed 1's interval. This is the
single largest mitigation for the compressed tail, and it costs no lesson time —
it is a constraint on texts the course was writing anyway.

### Seed 3 — condizionale di cortesia (U3 → analysed U11)

`vorrei` and `potrebbe` are unavoidable in any transactional text from U3
onward. Rather than pretend they are absent, they are named as chunks at
`U3 L1` and recur in U6's market dialogues and U7's office dialogues, so the
conditional's *politeness function* is familiar eight units before its
morphology is taught at `U11 L2`.

| Chunk | Seeded from |
| --- | --- |
| `vorrei` | `U3 L1` |
| `potrebbe` / `potresti` | `U3 L1` |
| `mi piacerebbe` | `U4 L1` |
| `sarebbe meglio` | `U9 L1` |

## Deliberately deferred

Referenced from [`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md`
§Unit 11](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md#unit-11--con-garbo-viaggi-ospitalità-richieste).

Structures a reader might expect and will not find, with the reason. Each is a
**cognitive load** or **communicative payoff** call, not an oversight.

| Deferred | Where it would have gone | Why it is out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Congiuntivo imperfetto** and the unreal conditional (`se avessi…, andrei`) | U11 or U12 | B2 territory. Adding it would mean two congiuntivo tenses in the last unit, breaking one-heavy-point-per-unit at exactly the moment learners are least able to absorb it. The real conditional (U10) covers the payoff cases at B1. |
| **Passato remoto** | U5 | Near-zero spoken payoff for a northern/central-oriented learner, and confined to written narrative. Seeded as *recognition only* in U12's news texts, where it genuinely appears; never produced. |
| **Congiuntivo passato** | U12 | Same argument as congiuntivo imperfetto. The first look at the present is the honest B1 claim. |
| **Futuro anteriore** | U10 | Low frequency, and the futuro epistemico already gives learners the speculative reading that carries most of the payoff. |
| **Pronomi relativi `il quale` / `cui` with prepositions beyond `in`/`con`/`di`** | U3 | Register too formal for B1 production. Recognition only in U12's media texts. |
| **`gerundio` as a subordinator** (`essendo…`, `avendo…`) | U12 | Written-formal. Would compete with the connettivi argomentativi for the same slot and lose on frequency. |
| **Concordanza dei tempi** as an explicit system | U9 | Taught implicitly through trapassato's use in U9 and U11. Making it an explicit topic at B1 buys metalanguage, not fluency. |
| **`ne` as a pronoun of origin/cause** (`me ne vado`, `non ne posso più`) | U6 | Taught as lexical chunks in U9 and U11 rather than as a paradigm — the paradigm is B2 work. |

A deferred structure is **not** the same as a forbidden one. Any of these may
appear in input at any point; what is deferred is *analysis and production*.

## Spacing audit

Under **distributed practice**, intervals should widen as mastery grows. Here is
what the course actually delivers.

### Where spacing is healthy (units 1–10)

Seventeen of the twenty-nine structures have both intervals at **two units or
more**, and eleven widen or hold flat. The strongest spirals are the ones the
course most depends on:

| Structure | Spacing | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passato prossimo | 4, 4 | The single most load-bearing structure in the course; widest available spiral. |
| `ci` locativo | 4, 4 | |
| Preposizioni | 4, 4 | |
| Relativi `che`/`cui` | 3, 4 | Widening. |
| Comparativi | 3, 4 | Widening. |
| `si` impersonale | 3, 5 | Widening. |
| Past-time expressions | 3, 5 | Widening. |
| `piacere` family | 7, 3 | Longest seeding interval in the course. |

### Where spacing is tight, and why it is accepted

| Structure | Spacing | Judgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Riflessivi** | 3, 1 | The `U5 L5` anecdote is genuinely the best free-production home for `mi sono accorto` / `mi sono spaventato`; moving it later would mean using a worse task to satisfy an interval. Riflessivi are also A2 **consolidation** — the learner arrives half-owning them, so the spiral is refreshing, not building. Accepted. |
| **Imperativo** | 1, 3 | `U7 → U8` is deliberate, not accidental: clitic *attachment* is easiest to learn while the pronouns are still fresh (map, U7 recycles). The second interval widens properly. Accepted. |
| **Pronomi indiretti** | 1, 2 | `U9` has to follow `U8` because combined pronouns *are* the interlock of direct and indirect. The alternative — separating them — would make U9 unteachable. Accepted, and mitigated by seven units of `piacere`-chunk seeding beforehand. |
| **PP vs imperfetto** | 3, 2 | Narrows slightly. Offset by the sheer number of extra encounters (U5, U8, U9, U10). Accepted. |

### Where spacing genuinely fails

Everything introduced from `U10` onward. This is the compressed tail, and it is
a container problem, not a sequencing mistake:

| Structure | Spacing | Shortfall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Futuro semplice | 1, 1 | Three encounters in three consecutive units. |
| Condizionale presente | 1, `M2` | Second encounter one unit later; free production only at the milestone. |
| `ci` pronominale | 1, `M2` | Same. |
| Periodo ipotetico / futuro epistemico | 2, `M2` | Same. |
| Pronomi combinati | 2, `M2` | Same. |
| Trapassato prossimo | 2, `M2` | Same. |
| **Congiuntivo presente** | — | **Two encounters, not three.** `U12 L2` and `M2`. There is no later unit. |
| **Connettivi argomentativi** | — | **Two encounters, not three.** Same reason. |

**Seven structures reach free production only at Milestone 2, and two never get
a third encounter inside the course at all.** That is the honest statement of
what a twelve-unit container buys.

It is mitigated three ways — receptive seeding (above), Unit 12 `L4` reserved
for whole-course recycling, and **washback**: Milestone 2 is weighted toward the
late structures precisely because learners study what gets tested. It is not
*fixed* by any of them.

The fix, if the board wants one, is **14 units** — recommended and escalated in
[`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md`
§5](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md#5-deliberate-compression-at-the-end-of-the-course-stated-plainly),
open for Chief Italian Linguist. Compressing more into Unit 12 is not the fix;
it trades **cognitive load** for **distributed practice** at a net loss.

## What this hands to ITA-4

Milestone 2 is not optional cover for the tail — it is the **third encounter**
for seven structures and the **second** for two more. That converts a design
preference into a hard requirement on the assessment:

**Milestone 2 must actively elicit, not merely permit:**

1. `condizionale presente` — a task the learner cannot complete politely without it.
2. `pronomi combinati` — a task where repeating full noun phrases would be unnatural.
3. `trapassato prossimo` — a narrative with a genuine anterior event to report.
4. `periodo ipotetico della realtà` and `futuro epistemico` — a speculation task.
5. `ci` pronominale — hard to force; accept at recognition level plus credit in free production.
6. `congiuntivo presente` after opinion verbs — **at recognition plus supported
   production only.** An item demanding unsupported congiuntivo at B1 is a
   **construct validity** failure: it would be testing B2.
7. `connettivi argomentativi` — a structured-argument task, marked for cohesion.

**Every unit checkpoint** must test at least one recycled structure from an
earlier unit — the per-unit `L4` contract above says which ones are available.

**Milestone 1** (after U6) covers only structures whose introduction is at or
before `U6`: presente, riflessivi, passato prossimo, past-time expressions,
relativi, comparativi, `ci` locativo, preposizioni, imperfetto, `si`
impersonale, PP/imperfetto contrast, `stare + gerundio`, connettivi narrativi,
pronomi diretti, `ne`, quantificatori. Nothing later.

## Verification

**Every recycle point resolves to an earlier introduction.** Checked
structure-by-structure: no row in the master table has a recycle unit ≤ its
introduction unit, and no row has a free-production unit ≤ its recycle unit.
`M2` sits after U12 and is therefore later than every unit.

**Every structure in the brief's required list is present:**
passato prossimo (U2) · imperfetto (U4) · the contrast (U5) · riflessivi (U1) ·
comparatives (U3) · futuro semplice (U10) · condizionale presente (U11) ·
pronomi diretti (U6) · pronomi indiretti (U8) · pronomi combinati (U9) · `ci`
(U3 locative, U11 pronominal) · `ne` (U6) · `si` impersonale (U4) and passivante
(U7) · trapassato prossimo (U9) · congiuntivo presente after verbs of opinion
(U12).

**Every structure has three located encounters** except the two named in the
spacing audit as having two. There is no structure in this document with only
one encounter.
