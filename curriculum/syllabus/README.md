# Syllabus

The A2→B1 syllabus map lives here: the full 12-unit grid with, for each unit,
its theme, can-do goals, grammar focus, lexis set, and the points where earlier
structures are recycled.

## The three documents

Read them in this order. The map is the contract; the other two are the
machinery that makes its claims checkable.

- [`a2-b1-syllabus-map.md`](a2-b1-syllabus-map.md) — the grid, readable in one
  sitting. Twelve units: theme, CEFR can-do goals, grammar focus, lexis set,
  pronunciation point, culture note, production task, recycling points. Plus the
  design decisions behind the sequence.
- [`grammar-progression.md`](grammar-progression.md) — all 30 structures with
  their three encounters located by unit *and lesson slot*, the per-unit `L4`
  recycling contract and how to read its "extended to" entries, the four
  cross-unit seeding strands (three receptive, one productive), the deferred
  structures, and a spacing audit that states where the twelve-unit container
  falls short.
- [`lexis-progression.md`](lexis-progression.md) — 12 sets × 30 items = 360,
  with the frequency rationale and interlock note for each set, the
  cross-course strands (`piacere`, connectives, false friends) with the rule for
  counting them, and the deliberate re-entries.
