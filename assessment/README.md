# Assessment

How we know a learner has actually reached B1. Task ITA-4, owner: Curriculum
Architect. All five files are written and **in review**; none contains
learner-facing Italian yet — every text, script, stem, key and anchor is a
specification for the Italian Content Editor.

| File | What it is | When | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`rubrics.md`](rubrics.md) | The five shared productive scales (TA, TS, GA, CC, FD), the fixed meaning of band 3 as the pass band, aggregation floors, marker calibration and the spelling policy. Every instrument below uses these scales and no others. | — | — |
| [`placement-check.md`](placement-check.md) | Diagnostic that places a learner in Band 1 (not yet ready), Band 2 (start at Unit 1) or Band 3 (start at Unit 7, after a confirmation step). No pass mark. Must be in use before Unit 1 is taught. | Before Unit 1 | ≈ 46 min, 60 marks |
| [`unit-checkpoint-template.md`](unit-checkpoint-template.md) | One ten-minute end-of-unit check, instantiated twelve times from a parameter table, with a worked Unit 6 example. A fail writes a carry-forward entry; it does not block the next unit. | Every unit, L5 | 10 min, 25 marks |
| [`milestone-1.md`](milestone-1.md) | Front-half milestone: past narrative, description and comparison, direct pronouns and `ne`, transactional speaking. Its Parts C–E double as the placement check's Band 3 confirmation step. | After Unit 6 | ≈ 77 min, 86 marks |
| [`milestone-2.md`](milestone-2.md) | The B1 exit, on CILS B1 / CELI 2 task types (original items). Elicits every structure whose third encounter is Milestone 2, as `grammar-progression.md` requires. | After Unit 12 | ≈ 163 min, 150 marks |

How they connect:

- The placement check's Band 3 is confirmed by Milestone 1 Parts C–E (64 marks,
  ≈ 50 min, ≥ 60% with no scale below 3). Changing either side means changing
  both.
- Checkpoint carry-forward entries close at later checkpoints or at Milestone 2;
  Milestone 2 is the only return point for congiuntivo and connettivi
  argomentativi.
- Placement, Milestone 1 and Milestone 2 results go back to the Chief Italian
  Linguist as the evidence that can reopen the twelve-unit decision
  (`docs/syllabus-shape-review.md`, Decision 1).

`assets/images/milestone-2/`) do not exist yet; each instrument states its
