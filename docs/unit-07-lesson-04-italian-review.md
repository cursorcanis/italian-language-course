## 1. The three points the Lesson Designer asked about

### 1.1 Factual claims in the culture note (as of 2026)

I checked every claim. All are true for Italy in general, and two needed a hedge
to stay true.

| Claim | Status |
| --- | --- |
| `codice fiscale` is needed for a phone, a bank account, the doctor | **True.** A SIM can't be activated without one, a bank won't open an account without one, and you can't register with the SSN (and so choose a `medico di base`) without one. |
| It is requested at the Agenzia delle Entrate and is free | **True.** It can also be issued by consulates abroad, and for non-EU citizens it is often assigned during the residence-permit procedure. A guide's generalisation can leave that out. |
| Without it, other offices "non possono fare niente" | **Overstated** → `quasi niente`. Some steps really are possible without it. |
| Residence is requested at the `anagrafe` | **True.** Since 2022 a change of residence can also be filed online through ANPR, but that needs SPID or CIE, which a newcomer won't have. The in-person route is the right one to teach. Not added to the text, for length. |
| A `vigile` may come round after a few weeks to check | **True.** The comune has 45 days to verify, and the check is done by the local police. `Dopo qualche settimana` is accurate. I added a gloss note: the official title is now `agente di polizia locale`, but everyone says `vigile`. |
| Many offices are by appointment only, booked online | **True in 2026.** Appointment systems became standard after 2020 at both the anagrafe and the Agenzia delle Entrate. |
| The marca da bollo isn't sold at the counter; you buy it at the tabaccaio | **True as a rule.** Some procedures now take the stamp duty digitally (pagoPA, `@e.bollo`), and the rule's wording still holds. The problem was the implication: the draft said "buy it before you go" as if every procedure needs one. **Neither procedure in this lesson does** — the codice fiscale and the residence declaration are both duty-free, and card 2 says so. Fixed: `Non tutte le pratiche la chiedono: se serve, comprala prima di andarci.` |
| The forms can be downloaded from "il sito del Comune" | **Too narrow** for a rule about offices in general (Agenzia forms come from the Agenzia's site) → `di solito si scaricano dal sito dell'ufficio`. |
| Ticket machine, a letter per service, number on the screen | **True.** Some offices now issue the ticket through an app as well. The text doesn't need to say so. |

Two related facts in stage 7, outside the culture note:

- **Paying a fine.** Fines are pagoPA notices today. They can be paid online, at
  the post office, at a tabaccheria or at a bank. A desk answering "Dove si
  paga?" with only `In posta` sounds like 2015. → `Online, oppure in posta.` The
  post office is still where the scene goes, so card 3 and the route are
  unchanged.
- **Documents for paying a fine.** You don't need ID to pay a notice at the post
  office. You need the notice, because the payment code is printed on it. The
  draft's `la multa … e un documento` taught a false requirement. → `Solo la
  multa: la porti con sé, perché sopra c'è il codice per pagare.` `la porti` is
  kept, so the exchange still models `Lei` + pronoun in front. Card 3 and the
  citizen's check-back were changed to match.
- **Residence documents** (card 1: ID, codice fiscale, rental contract): correct.
  A title to the property is required by law. **Codice fiscale documents**
  (card 2: passport and a copy, form filled in at the counter, free): correct.

### 1.2 `in Comune` vs `al Comune`

**Both are correct, and both are standard.** `In Comune` is the more common
everyday form, by the same analogy as `in banca`, `in posta`, `in questura`.
`Al Comune` is equally correct and slightly more neutral. Neither is an error,
and the lesson uses both: `Al Comune` in the map-key heading, ex 3 items 5 and 8
and ex 5 item 7; `in Comune` in the card 1 model clerk turn. I kept that mix.
It is what learners will hear.

The risk is in marking, not in the Italian. A teacher could penalise one form.
So I added one line under stage 4's preposition table: *both correct, both
common; accept either; never mark one of them wrong.* Capital `Comune` for the
institution or building is the normal convention, and it is consistent
throughout.

### 1.3 `Il numeretto lo prenda`

**Correct, natural, and worth keeping.** It is a left dislocation of the direct
object. The resumptive clitic is obligatory, so `Il numeretto prenda` would be
wrong. With a `Lei` imperative the clitic goes in front: `lo prenda`. This is
exactly how a clerk talks. It also does real teaching work: the fronted noun
makes the pronoun choice visible, and it is the `Lei` mirror of the ex 6 model's
`Il modulo scaricalo`. Two small edits in the model exchange: `Ah,` in front of
it, because the clerk is volunteering information nobody asked for and Italians
mark that shift; and a comma in `alla macchinetta, all'ingresso` in the card 1
turn. The other dislocations — `il contratto lo porti`, `il modulo lo compili`,
`la marca da bollo non la deve comprare` — are all correct and all kept.

## 2. Required fixes made in place

Each entry gives the lens, the change, and the reason.

**Culture text**

1. *Collocation naturalness.* `Vacci per primo` → `Vacci per prima cosa`. `Per
   primo` means *be the first person to go* (ahead of other people), not *go
   there first*. It is also masculine, which addresses only some readers. `Per
   prima cosa` is the idiom, and it is gender-neutral. `Vacci` is kept: it is a
   column 1 and column 4 sort item.
2. *Cultural accuracy.* `non possono fare niente` → `quasi niente` (§1.1).
3. *Collocation naturalness.* `è un fatto legale` → `ha un valore legale`. `Un
   fatto legale` reads as *a lawful fact*. The point is that residence has
   **legal force**.
4. *Cultural currency.* Rule 2: `Non tutte le pratiche la chiedono: se serve,
   comprala prima di andarci` and `di solito si scaricano dal sito dell'ufficio`
   (§1.1). `comprala`, `andarci` and `si scaricano` are all kept, so no sort item
   is lost. `se serve` is the same recognition chunk as rule 1's `se serve
   l'appuntamento`; I added it to the recognition note.

**Model directions**

5. *Orthographic precision.* `Supera il semaforo, e alla rotonda…`: the comma
   before `e` is removed.
6. *Spoken syntax.* `supera la rotonda, ed è sulla destra` → `supera la rotonda e
   la trovi sulla destra`. The draft left the subject of `è` hanging after a
   comma. `La trovi` is what people say, and it adds a free direct pronoun.

**Model exchange**

7. *Cultural currency.* `In posta.` → `Online, oppure in posta.` (§1.1).
8. *Discourse markers.* `Allora:` opens the route. The clerk had none, and a
   route at a desk always starts with one.
9. *Cultural accuracy.* `La multa — la porti con sé — e un documento` → `Solo la
   multa: la porti con sé, perché sopra c'è il codice per pagare.` The
   check-back now reads `porto la multa`.
10. *Discourse markers.* `Ah, il numeretto lo prenda…` (§1.3).

**Cards**

11. *Scene logic.* Cards 1 and 2 put the citizen at the bus stop **and** at an
    information desk. → `Sei allo sportello informazioni in Piazza della
    Stazione, accanto alla fermata del 12.` Every route still starts from the
    stop, and the clerk's `da qui` is now true.
12. *Register and address, collocation.* `Usa **Lei**` → `Dai del **Lei**`.
    `Rispondi in **Lei**` → `Rispondi dando del **Lei**`. *Lei* as a form of
    address takes `dare del`, or the article (`usa il Lei`). Bare `usa Lei` is
    not Italian. This is the wording learners should copy, so it matters.
13. *Cultural accuracy.* Card 3: `Portare: solo la multa (sopra c'è il codice per
    pagare)` and `Pagamento: online, oppure in posta: contanti o carta`, to match
    fix 9.
14. *Production coverage.* Flatmate checklist: `E il/la …? Lo/La porto?` → `E
    il/la/i/le …? Lo/La/Li/Le porto?`. Card 1 carries `documento`, `codice
    fiscale` and `contratto`, but card 2 carries `passaporto` and `una
    fotocopia`. The plural prompt lets a flatmate ask `E le fotocopie? Le porto?`
    and get `portale`, which serves R2-relayer criterion 4 ("the right
    `lo/la/li/le`").

**Model clerk turns**

15. *Discourse markers.* Card 1 opens `Allora, la residenza…`.
16. *Spoken syntax.* Card 2: `superi la rotonda.` stopped mid-route →
    `superi la rotonda ed è sulla destra.`
17. *Collocation naturalness.* Card 2: `Il numeretto è la lettera C` (a ticket is
    not a letter) → `Per il numeretto, scelga la lettera C`. This adds one more
    `Lei` imperative to the key.

**Exercise keys and items**

18. Ex 1 key: the quotations are re-synced to the edited text (`quasi niente`;
    `Si compra dal tabaccaio … se serve, comprala prima di andarci`).
19. Ex 3 key, item 6. *Grammar accuracy.* The note said "`stare` takes
    `essere`". In `Sei mai stato…?`, `stato` is **essere**'s participle (borrowed
    from `stare`, but it is the passato prossimo of *essere* = *to be/have
    been*). Telling learners it is `stare` plants a confusion that resurfaces
    every time `essere stato` comes back. → `` `sono stato/a` agrees with the speaker — U2 ``.
20. Ex 4 part A key, item 3: accept also `I documenti si possono ritirare…`. It
    is the more literal rewrite of `Potete ritirare`, and it is correct.
21. Ex 5 item 7. *Error-clinic integrity.* `Il Comune? Ci vada…` → `Al Comune? Ci
    vada…`. A bare hanging topic picked up by `ci` is acceptable in speech, but
    the key says this item is **correct**, and a sharp learner can argue that
    `ci` needs `a` + place to resume. A "correct" item must be beyond argument.
    The change also matches ex 3 item 8 and ex 4 B6.

**Ex 6 model message**

22. *Spoken syntax / collocation.* `Prima però prenota l'appuntamento: si prenota
    online` used the same verb twice in one breath. → `Prima, però, devi prendere
    l'appuntamento: si prenota online, sul sito del Comune.` The `si` rule is
    unchanged, so criterion 3's example still stands. Also `Oggi sono stato
    all'anagrafe` (time adverb first is the natural message opening) and a
    semicolon in the Comune/anagrafe pair. Now 97 words.

## 5. Suggestions (not applied — the author's call)

1. **`Cittadino` as the speaker label.** The citizen in this scene is a
   newcomer, often not an Italian citizen. Offices call members of the public
   `utente`. Either label works for learners. `Utente` is the more accurate one.
2. **`appare sullo schermo` → `compare sul display`.** `Compare` is the commoner
   verb for a number coming up, and `display` is what people call the screen.
   The draft is correct.
3. **`per il telefono` → `per una SIM`.** More precise (the SIM is where the
   legal requirement bites), but the draft is how people say it.
4. **Exercise 2 key, column 2.** `dal tabaccaio` (`si compra dal tabaccaio`) is
   position, not movement, and the key's own note says so for `all'Agenzia`.
   Labelling it "position" too would be consistent. Content, not Italian, so it
   is the Lesson Designer's call.
5. **Ex 5 item 5, `vado al medico`.** This is a regional colloquial form, common
   in parts of central and southern Italy. Marking it wrong for standard Italian
   is right. A teacher note saying learners may *hear* it would prevent an
   argument. Lesson Designer's call where it goes.
