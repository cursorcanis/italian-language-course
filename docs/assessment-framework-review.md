## Verdict

**Accepted.** The framework does what `docs/syllabus-shape-review.md` Decision 1
required of it.

- **Milestone 2 *elicits* the tail structures.** Each of the seven requirements
  has a scored floor and objective items, set out in a table that can be checked.
- **Congiuntivo is capped at 9 of 150 marks.** The U12 `L4` recycling list
  carries most of the marks, so U12 cannot be turned into congiuntivo practice.
- **Every instrument has a "What goes back to the Chief" section.** That makes
  the twelve-unit decision falsifiable in practice, not just on paper.

The link between the placement check and Milestone 1 holds:
- Parts C–E are 64 marks and about 50 minutes.
- The threshold is ≥ 39/64 (60%) with no productive scale below 3.
- I checked both documents myself.

All Italian in the milestones is a `⟨…⟩` specification. Nothing ships until
the Italian Content Editor writes it. The **placement check is written first**,
because it must ship before Unit 1 is taught.

## Rulings on the Architect's open items

| Item | Ruling |
| --- | --- |
| A learner who fails M1 badly (three or more scales below 3) | **Continues.** They carry a forward list into Units 7–8 and resit the failed parts at the U8 checkpoint. Repeating Units 5–6 is offered as a choice, never imposed. This is the same no-blocking rule as the unit checkpoints. A course that stops weak learners at the halfway point loses them. |
| `unit-01` says "do not move on" below 18/25; the template says a fail does not block | **The template wins.** Unit 1 is aligned to it under ITA-12's checkpoint step (Lesson Designer). |
| Thresholds for reopening Decision 1 | **Accepted as proposed**, provisionally. They are reviewed against the first real cohort's data. |
| Passed M2 sections stay valid for six months | **Accepted.** |
| CILS/CELI section names, formats and pass rules | **Not yet verified.** They were mapped from the Architect's own knowledge. Open task ITA-17: check them against the publishers' current specifications before any learner-facing material names them. |
| What "not yet" at M2 offers beyond section resits | **Open; goes to the user.** It is part of what the course promises learners. Until then the answer is "section resits only". |
| Using M2 for a cohort decision without an inter-marker step | **No.** Any cohort-level use needs a second marker on at least the productive parts. Individual use is fine. |
