## Suggestions — the author's call

1. **Ex. 4.3:** `che il lavoro non è per lui`. The present tense is defensible, since it is still true and the imperfetto is U4. `quel lavoro` would be clearer than `il lavoro` (which reads as "work in general"). The fully idiomatic form is `non fa per lui`.
2. **Ex. 6.6:** `Non ho visto il film: ho letto il libro`. The colon is fine, but `…il film, ma ho letto il libro` reads more naturally aloud.
3. **Set A′ example:** `Non mi sono mai pentito.` The native default is `non me ne sono mai pentito`. Keep it without `ne` at A2, but don't drill it as the only form. Matching ex. 4's `…della scelta` would be consistent.
4. **Self-study stage 8** (not Italian): "Cover Card B… say Pietro's answer from the note on Card B" asks the learner to read a card they have covered. Suggest: uncover one note at a time, build the sentence aloud, then check the key. Then do Nadia the same way from Card A. Owner: Lesson Designer.
