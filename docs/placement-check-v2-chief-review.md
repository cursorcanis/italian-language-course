## Required fixes: Italian Content Editor

1. **C2.4: v2's lure is stronger than v1's.** In v2 the gap follows a coordinated PP,
   `Ho pagato e (4) ___ (uscire) dal negozio quando…`. `Ho pagato e sono uscito` is a
   ready-made sequence chunk, and it pulls hard toward the rejected PP. In v1 the gap opens
   its own sentence (`(4) ___ (pagare) quando mi sono accorta…`). That makes the v2 item
   harder than v1's on the one construct where they must match. **Fix:** rebuild the
   sentence so the gap is not the second half of a PP coordination. One way is to move
   payment after the alarm, or to drop it, as long as the scene still makes clear the
   cuffie were paid for. Keep the contradicting clause (`Mi sono fermato sulla porta`).
2. **D2.1 v2: the key is the longest option.** "She was queuing for a ticket at the
   tobacconist's." is the longest of the three, which fails the ITA-25 checklist line "no
   multiple-choice key is the longest option". **Fix:** shorten the key, or lengthen a
   distractor. (v1 B1.1's key is also the longest, by one character: check it as well.)
