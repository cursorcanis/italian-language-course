## 2. Required fixes made in place, file by file

Each entry gives the lens, what changed, and why, so the pattern is learnable.

### Audio script

- **Cue 3 pairs and glosses** (orthographic precision; level grading). `àbito/abìto`
  became `càpito/capìto`, `ancòra` became `ancóra`, and the gloss table was
  rewritten. The pre-review explains why. The gloss table now has five rows
  that are all true.
- **Cue 3 order** (the cue's own spec). Part 1 read every pair A-then-B. That
  made the discrimination task solvable by position, and it broke the cue's own
  "random order" requirement. The new order mixes the pairs, and no pair's two
  members are adjacent. The L3 key was rebuilt to match.
- **Cue 3 accent note** (pre-review 2.3). Added. It also says that `pàpa` is
  spelled `papa` in ordinary writing.
- **Cue 2, Marta — `Mi manca quando va a casa dei suoi`** (spoken syntax;
  ambiguity). Coming straight after *Il suo fidanzato viene spesso a cena*, it
  reads as **she misses the boyfriend**. It is now `Quando lei va a casa dei suoi,
  un po' mi manca.` The chunk is intact, and `lei` fixes the referent.
- **Cue 2, Lina — `Non siamo amiche, siamo conoscenti`** said of a colleague
  (collocation and lexical accuracy). In Italian, a colleague you see every day is
  `una collega`, even if you are not friends. `conoscente` is for someone you know
  and have **no other name for**. As written, the cue that defines `conoscente`
  for the unit modelled it wrongly. `conoscente` now goes to the neighbour (`Non è
  un amico, è un conoscente → ma mi interessa parlare con lui…`), and Paola stays
  a colleague (`Non siamo amiche, però: ci vediamo solo in ufficio`).
- **Cue 2, Lina — `Mi piace il mio palazzo.`** (discourse markers). This
  sentence was tacked on to meet the checklist, and it sounded like it. It is now
  `Insomma, mi piace il mio palazzo.` — a wrap-up a person would actually say.
  `È un po' permaloso, questo sì` gives the concession a spoken shape.
- **Cue 4 — `Ci vediamo ogni giorno in ufficio`** (factual consistency). Elena
  teaches in a school, so it is now `a scuola`.
- **Cue 4 — final `↗` on `litighiamo solo per le vacanze`** (prosody). A final
  rise on a statement makes it a question. The mark is now `↘`, and the note
  says to put the affection in voice quality, not contour.
- **Cue 1 — `E tu↗ Com'è la tua↗`** (prosody; orthography). A wh-question
  falls in Italian, so it is now `E tu? ↗ Com'è la tua? ↘`, with a new note 6.
  The question marks were also missing, both here and in `[1]`.
- **Cue 1, note 1** (prosody). The rationale was wrong: it said the rise means
  she is "quoting Mark's question back". `vuoi sapere com'è la mia settimana?` is
  a plain yes/no question, and the rise is what makes it one. That ties straight
  to L4 stage 4. The note now says this.
- **Marking conventions.** "Rising contour, from the syllable that follows" was
  ambiguous about where the rise sits. Both arrows are now defined as carried by
  the last stressed syllable before the mark.
- **Cue 4** gained `↗` on Elena's two self-posed questions. Learners imitate
  this model, so the signposting contour should be marked.

### Overview

- §5: the pairs, `ancóra`, the accent note, and the "parole tronche" label (see §1).
- §2: `il dott. Ferri` became `l'ing. Ferri` (see L4).

### Lesson 1

- **Ex 4 item 3** (key validity). `La mattina ______ e poi faccio colazione` took
  `mi vesto` as well as `mi faccio la doccia`: getting dressed and then having
  breakfast makes perfect sense. The key's justification was false. The item now
  reads `…con l'acqua bella calda, e poi…`, which leaves only the shower, and the
  note was rewritten.
- **Ex 4 items 7/8** (key validity). `mi riposo` and `mi rilasso` fit both
  sentences. The key now accepts them either way round.
- **Teacher note 3.** Added that `Marco mi sveglia alle sei` is real Italian
  (*Marco wakes me up*).

### Lesson 2

- **Ex 4 item 3, error clinic** (learner-error anticipation; A8 integrity).
  `*Mia sorella mi alza alle nove` is not wrong Italian. It means *my sister gets
  me up at nine*, so an unflagged reading is correct, and a learner who ticks it
  is right. The item is now `*Mia sorella mi occupa della spesa`. It is the same
  error type (pronoun not following the subject), but the transitive reading is
  impossible. The teacher-notes row was changed to match.
- **Stage 2 Q4.** One sentence was added: `Marco mi sveglia` is Italian with a
  different meaning. That is the strongest argument for rule 2 the lesson has.
- **Stage 7** (grammar accuracy). "With `noi`, `voi` and `loro` it **usually**
  means *each other*" is false: `ci alziamo alle sette` is not reciprocal. It now
  says "can", and "almost always" only for `vedersi` / `sentirsi`.
- **Ex 3 key** (unchecked claim). Removed the assertion that `possiamo vederci`
  is "marginally more frequent in speech". I cannot stand behind it, and it cuts
  against the stage's "two equal options" line.
- **Ex 6 item 7** (item validity). The gap followed `devo`, so the key's
  alternative, `mi devo preparare`, could not fit. The item is now `(dovere /
  prepararsi, io)`, like items 8 and 9.

### Lesson 3

- **Stage 8 step 2** (orthography and prosody). The penultimate-stress examples
  were wrongly stressed: `ca-SA`, `ri-SER-va-to`. Teaching the default rule with
  examples that break it is the worst possible failure for this stage. They are
  now `CA-sa`, `pa-RO-la`, `ri-ser-VA-to`. The CCQ answer "three syllables
  earlier" for `abitano` was also wrong; it now reads `À-bi-ta-no`.
- **Ex 6 parts A and B.** New pairs, the accent note, a rebuilt key in the new cue
  order, and `an-có-ra`. Answers are now written as pair + letter (`3A`), because
  learners hear ten items from five pairs.
- **Ex 3 item 1** (level; seed discipline). `non le piacciono le feste` put the
  analysed third-person `piacere` in front of learners, which is U8 morphology,
  in the very lesson that seeds the chunks unanalysed. It is now `non va mai alle
  feste`.
- **Ex 3 item 3** (grammar). `su suo lavoro` is ungrammatical. The item is now
  `Se scherzi sul suo lavoro`.
- **Ex 7 model** (level; lexis accuracy). `non scherzare` became `meglio non
  scherzare` (pre-review 4.3). Gianni the colleague is no longer called a
  `conoscente`: the line is now `siamo solo colleghi`.
- **Teacher note 1 and stage 10.** The starred example `*Mia coinquilina è
  socievola` had two errors, because the article was also missing. An error
  example must show only the error it names, so it is now `*La mia coinquilina è
  socievola`.

### Lesson 4

- **`Dott. Marco Ferri` became `Ing. Marco Ferri`** (cultural accuracy). He says
  `sono ingegnere`, and an Italian engineer signs as `Ing.` The lesson's own
  culture note teaches `ingegnere` as a title, so the profile now demonstrates
  the note instead of contradicting it. This was changed in the profile, the
  round-2 heading, the ex 1B key and the overview.
- **`Quando si trova meglio per parlare — la mattina o la sera?`** (collocation
  naturalness). `trovarsi meglio per parlare` is not something anyone says. It is
  now `Preferisce sentirci la mattina o la sera?`, which is A2 and recycles the
  reciprocal.
- **Culture note** (cultural accuracy). "`tu` is the default … in most of the
  service situations … A barista will say `tu` to you" overstates the case.
  Plenty of baristas say `Lei` to adult customers. The text now says that it
  varies with age. "Anyone you have been introduced to socially" is now
  restricted to people of roughly your age. `Possiamo darci del tu` gained its
  question mark.
- **Teacher note 2** (error accuracy). `*Fai tu colazione al bar?` is grammatical:
  it means *is it **you** who has breakfast at the bar?* It is now
  `*Che cosa tu fai il sabato?`, and the note says not to star the other
  sentence if it is heard. It also adds that `Dove abiti tu?` is correct.
- **Stage 4(c)**: the bold on `svegl**i**?` now marks the ending, not the
  question mark.

### Checkpoint

- **A2** (one-answer rule). `Lavoro con lui ogni giorno, ma non è un amico: è un
  ______` accepted `conoscente` as well as `collega`. It is now `Lavoriamo
  insieme, nello stesso ufficio, tutti i giorni: è un mio ______`, and a marking
  note explains the A2/A3 split.
- **B5** (item validity). This had the same `devo ______` problem as L2 ex 6
  item 7, with the same fix.
- **Spelling note.** The example was backwards and showed an accent, not a
  letter. It is now `*sociavole` for `socievole`.

### Lexis

- `permaloso`: changed to `Meglio non scherzare…` (pre-review 4.3).
- `conoscente`: the example is now about a neighbour (`ci salutiamo e basta`). The
  note now says what the word is **not** for, and its "uses it constantly" became
  "far more readily".
- `sentirsi`: `anche solo un messaggio` became `anche solo con un messaggio`.
- The `piacere` chunks were listed as met in `L4 s3`, which contains none. That
  is now `L3 s4` and `s7`.
- `càpito` was added as a recognition-only item, and the count line was updated
  from 3 others to 4.

### Teacher notes

- Error row 3 was changed to match the L2 clinic. It now also warns that, with
  most of these verbs, the wrong pronoun makes a *different correct sentence*,
  so the correction is "not what you meant", not "not Italian".

## 6. Template sync (outside my lane — exact replacement text)

**`curriculum/units/unit-template/unit-NN-overview.md`, lines 142–147.** Replace
with the following. It also fixes `*abìtano`: the penultimate error is
`*abitàno`.

```markdown
- `-ano` third-person plurals — `àbitano`, `telèfonano`, `svégliano` — where an
  English-L1 learner reliably stresses the penultimate by analogy and produces
  *abitàno*.
- **Parole tronche** — words with obligatory final stress, where the written
  accent is part of the spelling: `caffè`, `perché`, `città`, `però`, `così`,
  `papà`, `lunedì`.
- Meaning-bearing pairs: `papà` / `pàpa`, `càpito` / `capìto`,
  `ancóra` / `àncora`, `princìpi` / `prìncipi`, `sùbito` / `subìto`.
- Accents on a non-final syllable (`àbito`, `svégliano`) are a reading aid for
  this exercise and are never written in ordinary Italian. Accents on a final
  syllable (`caffè`, `perché`, `papà`) are part of the spelling and are always
  written.
```
