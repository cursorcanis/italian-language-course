## 1. Cue 1 first, then the deferred L2 re-check

**Cue 1** (`Un anno di svolte`): **pass with one required fix.**

- **Count verified.** There are 28 passato prossimo forms, in the order of L1
  exercise 3's key: [1] 2 · [2] 4 · [3] 5 · [4] 6 · [5] 3 · [6] 3 · [7] 3 · [8] 2.
  16 use `essere`, across all four endings. There is no imperfetto, no
  congiuntivo, and no imperative with an attached pronoun.
- **Required (R-A1):** in [5], `l'agenzia ha deciso di assumere…` had no
  antecedent. The internship in [3] was `un tirocinio a Milano`, and no agency had
  been mentioned. A definite article needs something already known. [3] now
  reads `un tirocinio in un'agenzia di comunicazione a Milano`, which is the

  March key was updated to match. *Lens: discourse coherence; cultural currency.*
- **`E non è finita!`** (flagged by the Lesson Designer): **correct and natural.**
  It is the fixed spoken exclamation *and that's not all!*, and its null subject
  is idiomatic. L1's grid key glosses the hidden subject as *(la storia)*, which is
  right. It falls, as voice note 5 says.

## 3. Suggestions: the author's call

1. **Cue 1 [1], `ti ho scritto`.** Unit 1 cue 1 was a voice message, so
   `ti ho mandato un vocale` (or `ti ho raccontato`) would be strictly true.
   Everyday Italian uses `ti ho scritto` loosely for messaging. Changing it would
   ripple into L1's grid example and L2's signed-off board, so I left it.
2. **Cue 1 [7], `Mi sembra giusto così`.** This is fine. `Mi sembra meglio così`
   is what more people would say about a separation.
3. **`scorso` goes after the noun** (L3 stage 2, the lexis list): add "usually".
   `lo scorso anno` exists in writing and on the news.
4. **L4 culture note:** gloss `le pratiche` (*paperwork*). Exercise 1 item 3's key
   depends on it.
5. **L3 exercise 7 model:** `un anno pieno, ma vale la pena` reads better as `un
   anno faticoso, ma vale la pena`, because `ma` wants a contrast.
6. **L3 chat:** `E il tedesco?` arrives unmotivated. `E il corso di tedesco?` would
   anchor it.
