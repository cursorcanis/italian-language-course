# CILS B1 and CELI 2: verification of the Milestone 2 mapping

Owner: Curriculum Architect · Task: ITA-17 · Status: in review
Verifies: [`assessment/milestone-2.md`](../assessment/milestone-2.md) §Model, Section C intro, §Open
Closes the open item in [`assessment-framework-review.md`](assessment-framework-review.md) ("CILS/CELI section names, formats and pass rules: not yet verified").

**Method.** Every claim that `milestone-2.md` made about what the external exams *are* was checked against the publishers' own pages and PDFs: Università per Stranieri di Siena (Centro CILS) and Università per Stranieri di Perugia (CVCL). One secondary source was used, only to find a primary page. All sources were accessed on **2026-10-12**. Claims about the course's own marks, timings and pass rules are our design and were not "verified". The table below records them only where the file compared them with an external exam.

**What this file does not do.** It quotes no exam items, texts or prompts. It describes formats only: section names, number of tasks, task type, word ranges, timings and scoring rules.

**Which exam versions count.**
- **CILS:** the standard **UNO-B1**. CILS also runs a **B1 cittadinanza** variant, plus B1 adolescenti and B1 a. See [§B1 cittadinanza](#b1-cittadinanza-does-it-matter-here).
- **CELI:** the general **CELI 2 (B1)**. The CVCL page links both a "CELI 2" and a "CELI 2 a" description PDF; their text is the same apart from the title. Perugia also runs **CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza** for immigrants, which is not the model here.

**How current the sources are.** The CILS timing table is in the 2024 instructions PDF (modified May 2024). The CILS B1 sample paper and its scoring criteria are labelled "Sessione giugno 2017", but they are the current examples on the official page, and the writing sheets in the same set were reissued in 2025–26. The CELI 2 PDFs date from 2017–2019, and the current CVCL page still links them (page last updated 27-03-2025). **Residual risk:** neither publisher prints a "valid from" date on its format documents. Before learner-facing material quotes any figure from this file, re-check that figure.

## External formats as verified

### CILS UNO-B1 (Siena)

| Section (official name) | Tasks | Task types | Time | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Ascolto* ("Test di ascolto") | 3 | (1) 4-option multiple choice on several short texts; (2) 4-option multiple choice on one longer text; (3) information selection, true/false, on a radio broadcast | 30′ | 20 |
| *Comprensione della lettura* | 3 | (1) 4-option multiple choice on one text; (2) information selection, true/false; (3) text reconstruction: putting a text's shuffled parts back in order | 50′ | 20 |
| *Analisi delle strutture di comunicazione* | 4 | (1) cloze: articles and prepositions; (2) cloze: verb forms, with the infinitive given in brackets; (3) multiple-choice cloze; (4) multiple choice: matching an expression to its communicative situation | 60′ | 20 (from 24 raw) |
| (break) | | | 15′ | |
| *Produzione scritta* | 2 | (1) a set-topic text, 100–120 words (the current sample asks for a description or narrative of an event); (2) a set-topic text, 50–80 words (the current sample asks for an informal email to a friend) | 70′ | 20 |
| *Produzione orale* | 2 | (1) face-to-face conversation on a topic the candidate chooses, about 2–3 minutes; (2) one-way monologue on a topic or an image, about 1½ minutes | about 10′ | 20 |

Written papers total about 3 h 45′. The overall score is 100, and each skill is scored on its own. **To pass, the candidate must reach the sufficiency threshold in every skill.** A candidate who misses some skills receives a *capitalizzazione* certificate and may resit only those skills within **18 months**, at any centre. The numeric threshold per skill is not published on the pages accessed.

### CELI 2 (Perugia)

| Component (official name) | Tasks | Task types | Time | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Prova di comprensione della lettura* (Parte A) | 5 | A.1 short texts, 3-option multiple choice (7 items); A.2 one text, 10 binary-choice matchings; A.3 sentence completion, 4-option multiple choice (5); A.4 one text, 10 multiple-choice completions with 3 options; A.5 sentences to complete (5) | A + B: 2 h | 25% (scaled to 40) |
| *Prova di produzione di testi scritti* (Parte B) | 3 | B.1 a form or questionnaire (9 items); B.2 a short announcement to write or answer, about 50 words; B.3 a short letter or email following a given outline, 90–100 words | (in the 2 h) | 25% (40) |
| *Prova di comprensione dell'ascolto* (Parte C) | 3 | C.1 and C.2 short messages or news, 3-option multiple choice (4 + 4 items); C.3 two texts with binary-choice matching (25 items) | 20′ | 25% (scaled to 40) |
| *Prova di produzione orale* ("Prova orale") | 3 phases | an interview or conversation about oneself; a photo to describe, with simple evaluation of it; a role-play set by an image that is not described | 10–12′ (material handed over about 12′ before) | 25% (20 × 2 = 40) |

**CELI 2 has no separate grammar section.** Grammar and lexis are tested inside Parte A (A.4 and A.5). A separate *Prova di competenza linguistica* first appears at **CELI 3 (B2)**.

**Pass rule.** The candidate needs **≥ 72 / 120 on the written paper**: Parts A, B and C are summed, so they compensate for each other. The candidate also needs **≥ 22 / 40 on the oral**, and **≥ 94 / 160 overall**. Results are graded A–E, and A, B and C are passes. A pass in either the written paper or the oral is kept for one year (two sessions).

## B1 cittadinanza: does it matter here?

**No, apart from one sentence in M2.** CILS **B1 cittadinanza** ("B1 c") is a separate, shorter exam: about 2 h 05′ written, 4 skills, 2 listening tasks, 2 tasks of reading combined with *riflessione grammaticale*, 1 writing task (80–120 words) and 1 oral task. It is **valid only for a citizenship application**, and it **does not allow capitalizzazione**: a failed skill means resitting the whole exam (S9). The standard UNO-B1 is also accepted for citizenship, and its partial passes carry over (secondary source S-sec; the CILS FAQ, S10, says the B1 level is "richiesto anche per la cittadinanza"). Perugia's equivalent is CELI 2 i (S16).

M2 models the general exam, because the course's exit claim is a general B1. The cittadinanza variant tests a narrower range with fewer productive tasks, and its single-skill-fail rule is stricter, so it would be a worse model. The one practical consequence: a learner who intends to sit B1 cittadinanza should be told that it is shorter and differently grouped, and that a failed skill means resitting the whole exam. That is learner guidance and belongs to the Chief. It does not change M2.
